## Setup guide

#### Download from App Store

![Download from App Store demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step1.gif&w=828&q=75)

Install the official WeChat app from the iOS App Store - ensure it's by Tencent.

#### Download from App Store

Install the official WeChat app from the iOS App Store - ensure it's by Tencent.

![Download from App Store demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step1.gif&w=828&q=75)

#### Register Account

![Register Account demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step2.gif&w=828&q=75)

Launch WeChat, select 'Sign Up' and use your international mobile number. A friend with WeChat may need to verify you.

#### Register Account

Launch WeChat, select 'Sign Up' and use your international mobile number. A friend with WeChat may need to verify you.

![Register Account demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step2.gif&w=828&q=75)

#### Complete Verification

![Complete Verification demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step3.gif&w=828&q=75)

Navigate to 'Me' → 'Services' → 'Wallet'. Follow the prompts to upload passport photos and complete facial recognition.

#### Complete Verification

Navigate to 'Me' → 'Services' → 'Wallet'. Follow the prompts to upload passport photos and complete facial recognition.

![Complete Verification demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step3.gif&w=828&q=75)

#### Link Your Card

![Link Your Card demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step4.gif&w=828&q=75)

After verification, go to 'Wallet' → 'Cards' → '+' to add your international credit or debit card.

#### Link Your Card

After verification, go to 'Wallet' → 'Cards' → '+' to add your international credit or debit card.

![Link Your Card demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step4.gif&w=828&q=75)

#### Configure Security

![Configure Security demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step5.gif&w=828&q=75)

Set up a payment password and enable Touch ID or Face ID for secure and fast transactions.

#### Configure Security

Set up a payment password and enable Touch ID or Face ID for secure and fast transactions.

![Configure Security demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2Fwechat-ios-step5.gif&w=828&q=75)
