### **1. How to Take the Shanghai Pudong Airport Maglev Train**

The Maglev train from **Pudong International Airport** to downtown Shanghai is one of the most exciting ways to start your journey. This high-tech transport system can reach speeds of up to 300 km/h, offering a futuristic and thrilling experience. Here’s how to hop aboard and make the most of your Maglev ride: applicable for departures from both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2!

**Ticket Purchase Options**

Buying a Maglev ticket is straightforward, and you have several options:

- **Ticket machines**: Accept cash and UnionPay cards.
- **Ticket counters**: Accept cash and credit cards, including VISA, Mastercard, Discover, American Express, Diners Club, JCB, and UnionPay.
- Online : Via official WeChat accounts or other booking platforms
  - **Ticket Purchase**: [Trip.com Discounts](https://www.trip.com/t/HfTDkD95HP2)

**Ticket Prices:**

If you’re planning a return trip to the airport within 7 days, a round-trip ticket is more economical.

| Ticket Type | Price | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| One-way | ¥50 | Valid for the day |
| One-way (with flight) | ¥40 | Valid for the day |
| Round-trip | ¥80 | Valid within 7 days |

#### Maglev Train Operating Hours:

- **Pudong Airport to Longyang Road**: 07:02 – 21:42
- **Longyang Road to Pudong Airport**: 06:45 – 21:40

Trains run every 15-20 minutes, and the entire trip only takes 8 minutes! Be sure to check the schedule ahead of time to coordinate with your travel plans.

#### Transfer Options at Longyang Road Station

Once you arrive at Longyang Road, you have several ways to continue your journey into the city center:

- **[Subway Line 2](https://www.eastchinatrip.com/shanghai-metro-map-guide/)**: A direct transfer that takes you to [People’s Square](https://www.eastchinatrip.com/peoples-square-shanghai-travel-guide/), [East Nanjing Road](https://www.eastchinatrip.com/nanjing-road-shanghai-guide/), and other key locations.
- **Subway Line 7**: Ideal for reaching [Jing’an Temple](https://www.eastchinatrip.com/jingan-temple-shanghai-travel-guide/) and Changshou Road.
- **Taxis**: There is a designated taxi stand outside the station.
- **Buses**: Multiple bus lines are available for nearby destinations.

### **3. How to Take an Airport Taxi to Downtown Shanghai**

Taxis are a convenient and comfortable way to travel from **Pudong International Airport** to downtown Shanghai. If you’re carrying heavy luggage or prefer door-to-door service, this may be the best choice. Applicable for departures from both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2!

#### Taxi Guide

When you arrive at the airport, follow these steps to catch an official taxi:

1. Follow the airport signs to the designated taxi waiting area.
2. Queue in line—avoid accepting rides from unlicensed drivers.
3. Make sure to choose a licensed taxi with official markings.
4. Request the driver to use the meter to avoid overcharging.
5. Keep your receipt for any future queries or complaints.

## **Guide to Shanghai Pudong International Airport**

### **Tax Refund**

- Eligibility for Tax Refund :
  - Purchases made by the same foreign traveler on the same day at the same tax refund store must total at least 500 RMB.
  - The date of departure must be within 90 days of the purchase date.

- Refund Amount :
  - The refund rate is 13% of the invoice amount (including VAT), with 11% refunded directly to the traveler.

- Refund Process :
  - Before Security Check : Customs verification and confirmation. Follow airport signage for guidance.
    1. **T1 Terminal Customs Location**: Opposite Gate 11 in the departure hall. Service times align with flight schedules.
    2. **T2 Terminal Customs Location**: Opposite Gate 25 in the departure hall. Service times align with flight schedules.

  - After Security Check :
    1. **T1 Terminal**: Near Gates 19 and 20 in the international, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan departure area. Open 24/7.
    2. **T2 Terminal**: Near Gate D83 in the same area. Open 24/7.
    3. **Satellite Terminal S1**: Near Gates G135 and G152 in the same area. Open 24/7.

  - For more detailed information on tax refunds, visit our [China Tax Refund Guide](https://www.eastchinatrip.com/explore-china-travel-blogs-hotels-food-photography-tips/china-tax-refund-guide/).

### **Can You Stay Overnight at the Airport?**

Overnight stays are allowed at Shanghai Pudong International Airport, but there are no designated sleeping areas. Below are some recommended spots for rest:

#### **T1 Terminal**

- **Lounge H108**: After passing through the security checkpoint of the satellite hall, take the elevator down, ride the airport train, and find Lounge H108, which offers sofas.
- **Gate 15**: Located on the 3rd floor, near Gate 15. The seating is bench-style without armrests, making it suitable for lying down.

#### **T2 Terminal**

- **Gates C91–C98**: On the 2nd floor after the security checkpoint, sofas are available.
- **1st Floor After Security**: Seats are bench-style and without armrests, making them comfortable for reclining.

### **Connecting from International Flights to Domestic Flights**

When transferring from an international flight to a domestic flight at Shanghai Pudong International Airport, follow these steps to ensure a smooth transition:

1. Immigration and Customs Clearance :
  - Proceed to the immigration checkpoint for passport verification.
  - Collect your checked luggage at the baggage claim and go through customs.

2. **Re-check Your Luggage**: If your baggage is not checked through to your final destination, re-check it at the designated counters for your domestic airline.
3. **Security Check**: Pass through the domestic flight security checkpoint.
4. **Proceed to the Domestic Boarding Gate**: Follow signs to your terminal or gate. Be aware that transfers between terminals may require additional time.

### **Connecting from Domestic Flights to International Flights**

For transfers from domestic flights to international flights, follow these steps:

1. **Baggage Transfer**: If your airline provides through check-in service, your baggage will be transferred automatically. Otherwise, collect it at the baggage claim and re-check it at the international check-in counter.
2. **International Check-in and Immigration**: Check in for your international flight at the designated counter, then proceed to the immigration checkpoint for passport control.
3. **Security Check**: Complete the international flight security process.
4. **Proceed to the International Boarding Gate**: Follow the signage to the appropriate gate for your flight. Remember to allow extra time for security and passport control.

### **Boarding Gate Opening and Closing Times**

- **Domestic Flights**: Boarding gates typically close **40–45 minutes before departure**.
- **International Flights**: Boarding gates usually close **60 minutes before departure**.
