# How to Set Up Alipay as a Foreigner (2026) — Tour Pass Is Gone

**Download Alipay, link your Visa/Mastercard, and you're ready.** No Chinese bank account needed. Setup takes 5 minutes. Accepted at 90%+ merchants nationwide including street vendors, taxis, and restaurants. Transaction limits: ¥6,000 single / ¥50,000 monthly.

**"Tour Pass" was officially discontinued in March 2023.** Many 2019-2021 guides still mention it - ignore them. The current method is to directly link your international Visa/Mastercard to Alipay. This is simpler and works better.
