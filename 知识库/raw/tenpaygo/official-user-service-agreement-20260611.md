# TenPayGo Payment User Service Agreement

**Tenpay Payment Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company"), for the convenience of overseas users (hereinafter referred to as "you"), provides the payment service (hereinafter referred to as the "TenPayGo Payment Service" or "the Service") to you through the TenPayGo App, to facilitate your payments to merchants within Chinese mainland (which, for the purposes of this Agreement, excludes the Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Taiwan region of China). This Agreement is legally binding on both you and the Company. Before using the TenPayGo Payment Service, you shall read, sign, and accept this Agreement, the** [Tenpay Service Agreement](https://posts.tenpay.com/posts/3b020ca36008eb660046aad41d458236.html) **and the** [Tenpay Privacy Policy](https://posts.tenpay.com/posts/62fd7a3664e33216012f2c433495df43.html)**. As the TenPayGo Payment Service is provided by the Company relying on the TenPayGo App, you shall also read and comply with the TenPayGo Software License and Service Agreement. The Company hereby specifically reminds you to carefully read and fully understand the terms of the foregoing agreements, in particular the terms that exclude or limit the Company's liability, restrict your rights, or provide for dispute resolution methods. Please carefully read and consider and choose whether to accept the foregoing agreements (minors must read this Agreement under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian). If you have any questions about this Agreement, please consult customer service.**

**1. The Service**

1.1 The TenPayGo Payment Service refers to the monetary funds transfer service provided by the Company between payers and payees relying on the TenPayGo App. The Service is limited to your making consumption payments to merchants that meet the Company's requirements within Chinese mainland, using International Cards (as defined below) that you have bound to the Service, which are issued by banks established outside Chinese mainland (for the purposes of this Agreement, including the Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Taiwan region of China) (hereinafter referred to as "International Cards"), or an e-wallet established outside Chinese mainland (hereinafter referred to as the "Overseas Wallet"), and the Service does not include functions such as opening a payment account, topping up a payment account, transferring funds, receiving transfers, or sending and receiving red packets. The specific types of International Cards, Overseas Wallets, service scenarios, and specific functions supported by the Service shall be subject to the prompts on the service pages.

1.2 Service activation process: In order to activate the Service, you need to provide the Company with necessary personal information, which may include your real-name information, bank card information, and overseas wallet information; the specific information fields shall be subject to the page prompts. You also need to enter and verify the email address you bound when opening your International Card, as prompted by the page. After the verification is passed, we will then submit your information to the relevant overseas card-issuing bank or overseas wallet institution for verification. After the overseas card-issuing bank or overseas wallet institution passes the verification, the Company will activate the Service for you.

1.3 Service Usage Process: When you choose to make payment by binding an International Card on TenPaygo, you need to complete the payment in accordance with the TenPaygo page prompts. When you choose to make payment by binding an Overseas Wallet on TenPaygo, you will be guided to jump to the Overseas Wallet application, and the payment will be completed within the Overseas Wallet application.

1.4 You understand and agree that the above process of the Service may be adjusted due to changes in applicable scenarios and the continued existence of functions, and shall be subject to the service process actually provided by the Company.

1.5 Quota Description:

1.5.1 After you activate the Service, you may only make consumption payments within a certain quota, the specific amount of which shall be subject to the page prompts. If you need to increase your payment quota, please follow the page guidance.

1.5.2 In order to protect the security of your funds, during your use of the Service, the Company will set/adjust the transaction quota available to you based on factors such as changes in laws, regulations and regulatory policies, changes in payment business scenarios, and risk control needs; the specifics shall be subject to the prompts on the service pages. You also need to comply with the relevant quota rules of the overseas card-issuing bank or overseas wallet institution. If you are unable to use the Service due to payment restrictions of your overseas card-issuing bank or overseas wallet institution, or other business rule requirements, you may try to bind another International Card or Overseas Wallet under your name or use another payment method.

1.6 In order to comply with the operational requirements of Chinese laws and regulations regarding non-bank payment institutions, you authorize the Company, within the scope permitted by laws and regulations or upon obtaining your authorization, to query and verify your identity information and account information from Chinese regulatory authorities and financial institutions, international card organizations, overseas card-issuing banks, overseas wallet institutions, and others.

1.7 You understand and agree that the Company is entitled, within the scope permitted by law and in accordance with the needs of business operation adjustments, to revise the contents of this Agreement and to announce the latest service agreement in advance through the Company's website or the TenPayGo App. You may follow and learn about the latest service agreement announced by the Company. The latest service agreement takes effect 30 days after the announcement. If you are unable to agree to the contents of the latest service agreement, you are entitled to stop using the Service.

**3. Functions of the Service**

3.1 Consumption Payment – You may use the Service to purchase goods and services from merchants. The Company will, in accordance with your transaction instructions, debit the corresponding funds from the International Card bank account or the Overseas Wallet associated with you to the merchant's account.

3.2 Transaction Inquiry – The Company will record the transactions in which you use the Service, and you may view transaction records in the TenPayGo App. If you need to learn information on the Overseas Wallet side (including but not limited to fees related to the Overseas Wallet), please directly inquire with your Overseas Wallet.

3.3 Adding/Removing/Re-adding/Unbinding a Bank Account/Bank Card or Overseas Wallet – You may add a new International Card or Overseas Wallet in the TenPayGo App, and/or perform operations such as removing, re-adding, and unbinding; the specifics shall be subject to the prompts on the service pages.

3.4 Changing/Resetting the Payment Password – You may reset the payment password by verifying the bound International Card information or the email verification code; the specific method shall be subject to the Tenpaygo page prompts.

3.5 Refund – If you apply for a refund in respect of a TenPayGo payment transaction, after the refund is successful, the Company may directly return the funds to the original International Card bank account or the Overseas Wallet associated with you.
