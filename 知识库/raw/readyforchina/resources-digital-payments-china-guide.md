## Digital Payments Dominate China

China leads the world in mobile payments. From remote villages to luxury malls, cash and foreign credit cards are increasingly obsolete. Travelling without AliPay or WeChat on your smart phone is almost impossible, as the vast majority of merchants accept only mobile payments.

## The Two Major Platforms

![Alipay Logo](/alipaylogo.svg)

### Alipay (支付宝)

Owned by Ant Group (Alibaba affiliate)

**Best for:** Shopping, dining, transportation

**Tourist feature:**Dedicated "Tour Pass" for visitors

### WeChat Pay (微信支付)

Integrated into WeChat by Tencent

**Best for:** Peer-to-peer transfers, small businesses, social payments

**Social integration:**Built into China's top messaging app

When traveling to China, the choice between AliPay and WeChat Pay is often the biggest logistical hurdle for international visitors. While both platforms are now essential for a cashless experience, they serve slightly different purposes.

**AliPay**, operated by Ant Group, is generally considered the more "tourist-friendly" option because its international version allows users to link foreign credit cards with fewer verification hurdles than its competitor. It excels as a dedicated financial tool, offering superior exchange rate transparency and a streamlined interface for booking high-speed trains and hotels via integrated platforms like Trip.com.

The primary advantage of **WeChat**is it is the preferred method for "peer-to-peer" transfers (think Venmo, CashApp, PayPal) and is often the only way to pay at very small street stalls or for local services. However, the setup process for foreigners can be more complex.

Both platforms are widely accepted across China, but all tourists should start with AliPay.

Bottom line: AliPay is the most convenient for tourists and more widely accepted by merchants in Chinas. For most tourists, it is recommended to setup AliPay first.

## Alipay Setup Guide

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Download** Alipay from your app store
2. **Language:** Settings → General → Language → English
3. **Sign up** with international mobile number
4. **Select "Tour Pass"** (visitor feature)
5. **Verify identity** with passport
6. **Add foreign bank card**

## WeChat Pay Setup Guide

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Download** WeChat from app store
2. **Register** with international number
3. **Navigate:** Me → Services → Wallet
4. **Add card:**Tap "Cards" → Add foreign bank card
5. **Verify passport** and accept terms

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### When Payments Fail

#### Most Common Causes:

- • Unsupported card type (AMEX often rejected)
- • Incomplete identity verification
- • Merchant has domestic-only account
- • Network connectivity issues

#### Solutions:

- • Keep ¥200-500 cash as backup
- • Try switching between apps
- • Ensure identity verification is complete
- • Install VPN before arrival
