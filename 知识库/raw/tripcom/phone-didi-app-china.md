# How to Use DiDi App in China as A Foreigner

![Author's Profile Picture](https://dimg04.c-ctrip.com/images/1hq5i12000qjyufepA898.jpg)

By Leona

August 25, 2025

![DiDi App China](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M71012000nlqt8oy9F9C_Q50.jpg)

If you’re travelling around China, one of the easiest ways to get from place to place is by using DiDi, China’s version of Uber. The DiDi app makes it simple to book rides, avoid language barriers, and pay securely without needing to haggle with taxi drivers. For foreigners, especially first-time visitors, DiDi App is the best choice to save a lot of stress on the road.

## What is DiDi App in China?

![DiDi in China](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M71312000ly3dpu63A5B.png)

DiDi Chuxing, often just called DiDi, is the biggest ride-hailing app in China. It’s basically China’s version of Uber or Grab. Through the DiDi app, you can book different types of rides, from budget options and taxis to premium cars with professional drivers.

It’s available in most Chinese cities and has an English interface. And it’s easier for foreigners to get around without speaking Mandarin.

## How to Download and Set Up the DiDi App?

### Download the App

Search for “DiDi Ride-Hailing” on the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). In China, you can also download it from local app stores if Google Play isn’t available.

![DiDi Ride Hailing](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M74v12000ly3e1im4A94.png)

### Choose Language

The app has an English version. Once installed, you can switch to English in the settings for easier use.

Go to "我的"-“设置”-“多语言/Language”. Choose English and click "完成".

![DiDi Language Setting](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M73d12000ly3flfw6A78_Q60.jpg)

### Register an Account

Sign up with your mobile number (a local Chinese SIM card works best, but some foreign numbers also work). You’ll receive a verification code by SMS to log in.

![DiDi Login](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M73412000ly3fy3tCB24.png)

### Add Payment Method

Link WeChat Pay or Alipay for smooth payments. Some international credit cards are accepted, but local e-wallets are more reliable.

## How to Use the DiDi App in China?

### Set Pickup Location and Destination

The app will detect your location automatically. You can also adjust the pin if you’re standing somewhere specific (like a hotel entrance).

Type your address in English or Chinese. If you’re not sure, copy and paste the Chinese name from Google Maps or ask your hotel staff.

![DiDi Ride Hailing](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M72y12000ly3jur3EE5B_Q60.jpg)

### Choose Your Ride Type

- Express: Affordable, everyday option.
- Premier: Higher-end cars with professional drivers.
- Taxi: Standard metered taxi, paid through the app.
- Carpool (Hitch): Share a ride at a lower cost (less common for foreigners).

### Confirm Your Ride and Track

Check the estimated fare and confirm your ride. You can watch your driver’s route on the map and get updates on their arrival.

![DiDi Ride Hailing](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M70m12000ly3kgir9DC7_Q60.jpg)

### During the Ride

Always check the car plate number and driver’s photo before getting in. You can share your trip with friends or family for extra safety.

### After the Ride

Payment is automatic through your linked method. You can rate the driver or leave feedback if needed.

## Can Foreigners Use DiDi in China?

![Ride Hailing](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M75412000nlvjjhw56C6_Q50.jpg)

Yes. Foreigners can definitely use the DiDi app in China, but there are a few things to note:

- **Phone Number**: You need a valid mobile number to register. A China eSIM or SIM card with a local phone number is the easiest option.
- **Payment**: The most reliable methods are WeChat Pay or Alipay. While some foreign credit cards can be added, they may not always go through.
- **Language Support**: The app has an English version, so you don’t need to read Chinese to book rides.
- **Addresses**: You can type destinations in English, but sometimes drivers see them in Chinese. Copy-pasting the Chinese address from your hotel or map app helps avoid confusion.

For most travellers, once you have a local SIM and a linked payment method, using DiDi is as easy as booking a Grab or Uber back home.

## Alternatives to DiDi App in China

![Taxi Apps in China](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M77312000ly3chso4A19.jpg)

While DiDi is the most popular ride-hailing app, there are other options you can try if DiDi isn’t working or you want more choices:

| **Option** | **Language Support** | **Payment Options** | **Best For** | **Drawbacks** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DiDi | English & Chinese | WeChat Pay, Alipay, some cards | Most foreigners, easy to use | Needs setup, some drivers don’t read English |
| Meituan Ride-Hailing | Mainly Chinese | WeChat Pay, Alipay | Locals, those who already use Meituan | Not foreigner-friendly, no English UI |
| Gaode (AutoNavi) | Chinese only | WeChat Pay, Alipay | Locals needing map + ride in one app | App in Chinese, harder for tourists |
| Traditional Taxis | No app needed | Cash, sometimes Alipay/WeChat | Quick rides without internet | Language barrier, higher chance of overcharge |

## Tips for Using DiDi in China

![Getting Around Shanghai](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/10070x000000lwxof6B43_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

**Check the Car Plate**: Always match the license plate and driver info in the app before getting in.

**Use Chinese Addresses**: Copy the Chinese name of your destination from your hotel or map app to avoid confusion.

**Payment Ready**: Link WeChat Pay or Alipay in advance, since foreign cards may fail.

**Share Your Trip**: Use the app’s trip-sharing feature to let friends know where you are.

**Avoid Peak Hours**: Prices surge during rush hour (7–9am, 5–7pm). The metro may be faster.

**Stay Online**: You need mobile data for booking. A local SIM or eSIM makes things smoother.

## DiDi: Your Go-To App for Getting Around China

For travellers in China, the DiDi app is by far the most convenient way to get around. With an English interface, multiple ride options, and easy payments via WeChat or Alipay, it takes the stress out of navigating unfamiliar streets. Download and set it up before your trip, and you’ll have a reliable ride at your fingertips wherever you go.

## FAQs About the DiDi App China

- Do I need to speak Chinese to use DiDi?

 No. The app has an English interface, and you can enter addresses in English (though copying the Chinese address helps).
- Can I use DiDi without mobile data?

 No. You need an internet connection to book rides, track your driver, and pay. A local SIM or eSIM is recommended.
- How do I ensure safety when using DiDi?

 Always check the car plate and driver’s info in the app before getting in, and share your trip with friends or family.
- Are DiDi fares fixed or metered?

 It depends on the ride type. Taxis use meters, while Express and Premier have fixed prices shown in the app before you book.
- Can I cancel a ride on DiDi?

 Yes, but fees may apply depending on timing and city rules.
