# How to Book High-Speed Trains in China: 12306 vs Trip.com

China's high-speed rail network is the world's largest and most impressive, connecting major cities at speeds up to 350 km/h. Whether you're traveling from Beijing to Shanghai (4.5 hours), Shanghai to Hangzhou (1 hour), or exploring lesser-known routes, booking your tickets in advance is essential — especially during holidays.

## Price Comparison: 12306 vs Trip.com

The main difference between platforms is the service fee. Trip.com adds a small booking fee (typically ¥10-¥30 per ticket) . On longer routes, this amounts to a very small markup for the convenience of an English interface and international payment options. However for short commuter routes, the percentage markup can be more noticeable.

Here's how prices compare on popular routes:

| Route | 12306 Price | Trip.com Price | Ave. Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beijing → Shanghai2nd Class, 4.5 hours | ¥553 | ¥590-620 | +7% |
| Beijing → Xi'an2nd Class, 4.5 hours | ¥515 | ¥555-590 | +8% |
| Guangzhou → Shenzhen2nd Class, 30 min | ¥74 | ¥95-115 | +15% |
| Shanghai → Hangzhou2nd Class, 1 hour | ¥73 | ¥95-105 | +14% |

Percentage vs Absolute Cost

While Trip.com's percentage markup looks higher on cheap tickets, the **absolute cost difference**is often only ¥20-50 ($3-7 USD). On longer routes like Beijing-Shanghai, you're paying about $5-8 extra for English support and international card acceptance — often worth it for peace of mind.

### Step-by-Step Registration

#### Download the 12306 App

Available on iOS App Store and Android. Search for "铁路12306" (Railway 12306).

![12306 official store page](/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2F12306_official_app.png&w=3840&q=75)

## SCAM APP

Make sure you download the official app, scam apps that charge additional booking fees are common.

#### Register with Chinese Phone Number

You'll receive an SMS verification code. A local SIM card or eSIM with a Chinese number is required.

#### Complete Identity Verification

Enter your passport details exactly as they appear. Take a selfie for facial recognition verification.

#### Link Payment Method

Connect WeChat Pay or Alipay. International cards may not work directly.

#### Search and Book

Enter departure city, destination, and date. Select your train and seat class, then pay.

12306 Language Tip

The app has limited English support. Use Google Translate's camera feature to translate screens in real-time, or screenshot and translate. Key terms: 出发 (departure), 到达 (arrival), 二等座 (2nd class), 一等座 (1st class).

## Using Trip.com

Trip.com is the easiest option for international travelers. The entire booking process is in English with no Chinese phone number required.

### How to Book on Trip.com

#### Visit Trip.com Train Section

Go to Trip.com and select "Trains" from the menu. Choose "China Trains" for domestic routes.

#### Search Your Route

Enter your departure and arrival cities in English. Select your travel date and search.

#### Select Your Train

Browse available trains by departure time, duration, and price. G-trains are fastest (Gaotie), D-trains are also high-speed.

#### Enter Passenger Details

Enter your passport name and number exactly as shown. This information must match your physical passport.

#### Pay and Receive Confirmation

Pay with Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal. You'll receive an e-ticket confirmation via email.

## At the Train Station

Regardless of how you booked, you'll need to know how to navigate Chinese train stations:

### Collecting Your Ticket

#### Passport-Only Entry (Recommended)

Most major stations now support **passport-only boarding**. Simply scan your passport at the automated gates — no paper ticket needed.

Works at: Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, and most Tier-1 city stations

#### Collecting Physical Tickets

At smaller stations or if you prefer a physical ticket, use the self-service machines (scan passport) or visit the ticket window.

Tip: Arrive 30+ minutes early if you need to collect tickets at a window

### Boarding Process

1. 1**Security Check:** All bags go through X-ray. No large knives or flammable items.
2. 2**ID Check:** Scan your passport at the entry gates (same passport used for booking).
3. 3**Find Waiting Room:** Check display boards for your train number and gate. Format: G123 → Gate 4A.
4. 4**Boarding:** Gates open 15-20 minutes before departure. Scan passport again to enter platform.
5. 5**Find Your Seat:** Carriage number (车厢) and seat number (座位) are on your ticket/confirmation.

## Essential Tips for Train Travel

### Book Early for Holidays

During Chinese New Year, National Day (Oct 1), and Labor Day (May 1), trains sell out fast. Book as soon as the 30-day window opens.

### Check Station Names Carefully

Cities often have multiple stations. Shanghai has 4+ major stations. Make sure you're going to the right one (e.g., Shanghai Hongqiao vs Shanghai Station).

### Download Offline Content

WiFi on trains is inconsistent. Download movies, music, or maps before your journey. Cell signal can drop in tunnels.

### Food and Drinks

Food and snacks are available from trolleys that pass through the carriages. On G-trains and D-trains, you can also order meals for delivery to your seat through the 12306 app (requires [Alipay](/resources/alipay-setup-guide-2026)). Hot water dispensers are available free in each carriage.
