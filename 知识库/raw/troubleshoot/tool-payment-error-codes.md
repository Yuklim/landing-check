## Why Use This Tool?

Payment errors are a common frustration for travelers and expats using WeChat Pay or Alipay in China. Error codes and unclear messages can appear for many reasons:

- Card compatibility issues
- 3-D Secure or bank restrictions
- Risk controls or temporary system maintenance
