# Shanghai Pudong Airport Guide 2026: Terminals, Transport & Facilities

Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG) is huge, busy, and often the first stop for travellers arriving in China or passing through on a long-haul connection. Whether you’re landing late at night, transiting for a few hours, or starting your Shanghai adventure from here, a little preparation goes a long way!

## Shanghai Pudong Airport Overview

- Official name: Shanghai Pudong International Airport
- Airport code: PVG
- Location: Pudong New Area, around 30 km east of Shanghai city centre
- Terminals: Two main terminals - Terminal 1 and Terminal 2, and a massive Satellite Terminal (S1/S2)
- Popular airlines: China Eastern Airlines, Air China, Shanghai Airlines, China Southern Airlines, Singapore Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Emirates, Lufthansa
- Passenger traffic: Handled over 76 million passengers a year

## Shanghai Pudong Airport Terminals & Map

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Layout Map](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M77312000rg5qtep74CE_Q60.jpg)

**Source: China Airline Travel**

Shanghai Pudong Airport has two main terminals: Terminal 1 (T1) and Terminal 2 (T2). In addition, there are two satellite terminals: S1/S2, which serve as extensions of T1 and T2 respectively.

- Terminal 1 (T1): Older terminal on the west, slightly smaller, mainly serving domestic flights and selected international routes.
- Terminal 2 (T2): Newer and larger on the east, handling the majority of international and long-haul flights, including many full-service carriers.
- S1/S2 satellites link to T1/T2 via APM trains

### How to Get Between Terminals at Pudong Airport?

1. Free Inter-Terminal Shuttle Bus

- Operating hours: Roughly 24 hours
- Frequency: Every 10–15 minutes
- Travel time: About 10 minutes
- Where to board: T1/T2 arrival halls (1F)

1. APM Trains

- Operating hours: 6:00 AM–11:00 PM
- Frequency: 2–3 minutes
- Travel time: About 2 minutes
- Connection: T1→S1 and T2→S2

1. On Foot (Not Recommended with Luggage)

The terminals are technically walkable, usually takes 15–20 minute walk, but the distance is long and not ideal, especially with bags.

## Shanghai Pudong Airport Departure & Arrival

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Arrival & Departure](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M75p12000rg5oy2dDA69_Q60.jpg)

**Source: China Airline Travel**

What to expect when departing:

1. Arrival at terminal: Aim to arrive at least 3 hours before international flights. Walking distances can be long.
2. Check-in & baggage drop: Self-service kiosks are available for some airlines, but counters can get busy during peak hours.
3. Security screening: Standard airport security; liquids rules apply.
4. Exit immigration: Passport control is generally efficient but queues can form during evening departure waves.
5. Airside area: Duty free shopping, lounges, charging points and dining options.

What to expect when arriving:

1. Immigration clearance: Includes passport checks and fingerprint scanning for most foreign travellers.
2. Baggage claim: Clearly marked carousels; trolleys are free.
3. Customs inspection: Usually quick unless selected for inspection.
4. Arrival hall: This is where you’ll find taxis, airport buses, metro access and ride-hailing pick-ups.

💡 Tip: Ignore anyone offering “taxi” services inside the termina. Always use official taxi queues or apps.

## Shanghai Pudong Airport Check-in & Baggage Services

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Check in](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/1mh3m12000ifv5q6g5C51_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

### Check-in

Both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 have check-in counters for all airlines. Terminal 2 is larger and more modern. Counters usually open 3 hours before international flights and 2 hours before domestic departures.

Many airlines (China Eastern, Singapore Airlines, Lufthansa, etc.) offer self-service check-in. You can print your boarding pass and tag your luggage, then drop it at a dedicated bag-drop counter.

Tip: Self-service kiosks can save 10–15 minutes. If you fly on China domestic flights, remember to check your power bank because China requires a 3C certification now.

### Baggage Drop & Luggage Policies

- Standard luggage allowance: Check with your airline. Most international flights allow 1–2 checked bags up to 23–32 kg each.
- Excess baggage: Counters are available for payment; prices vary by airline.
- Baggage wrapping & trolleys: Available in both terminals. Wrapping stations usually cost around ¥20–¥40 (~S$4–S$8) per bag.

## What to Buy at Shanghai Pudong Airport?

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Shopping](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/1008190000015nts8AA1A_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

### Duty-Free Shopping

Location: Both Terminals 1 and 2 have extensive duty-free areas past security. Terminal 2 has the largest selection for international travellers.

Popular items:

- Luxury goods: Watches, handbags, jewellery, designer fashion.
- Cosmetics & skincare: Global brands like Estée Lauder, Lancôme, Shiseido, plus popular Asian skincare lines.
- Liquor & tobacco: Premium whiskey, wine, vodka, and Chinese baijiu.

### Local Snacks & Gifts

- Chinese tea: High-quality green tea, oolong, and pu-erh.
- Confectionery: Mooncakes, nougat, and traditional sweets make great gifts.
- Souvenirs: Panda-themed merchandise, keychains, silk products, and Shanghai-themed items.

### Electronics & Travel Essentials

Headphones, power banks, travel adapters, cameras, and some mobile accessories.

Tip: Prices may be slightly higher than city stores, so buy if it’s convenient or last-minute, not necessarily cheaper.

### Fashion & Accessories

Luxury boutiques: Gucci, Louis Vuitton, Cartier, Chanel, and more in Terminal 2.

## What to Eat at Shanghai Pudong Airport?

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Dining](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/1mh2v12000bm2t416C92E_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

- Noodles & Dumplings: Many fast-service restaurants serve hand-pulled noodles, wontons, and steamed buns.
- Rice & Set Meals: Bento-style dishes with rice, vegetables, and meat are common, especially in Terminal 2.
- Global chains: McDonald’s, KFC, Burger King, and Starbucks are available.
- Café: Starbucks, Pacific Coffee, and local cafés offer coffee, tea, and light snacks.
- Bakeries: Freshly baked pastries, cakes, and traditional Chinese buns.

## Facilities at Shanghai Pudong Airport

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Medical Service](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/1mh0n12000c93e1gy69C6_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

Currency Exchange & Banking: Both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 have multiple currency exchange counters and ATMs.

Tax Refund Counters (VAT Refund): After security and before departure gates (most international carriers). Show your passport, receipts, and completed refund forms at the counter.

Luggage Storage: Both terminals have 24-hour luggage storage and baggage wrapping services. Around ¥20–¥50 per item (~S$4–S$10) depending on size and duration.

Showers & Rest Areas: Many airline and pay-per-use lounges have showers and nap areas. Terminal 2 has dedicated shower rooms (~¥60–¥100 / S$11–S$19).

Mobile & Wi-Fi Services: Free Wi-Fi is available throughout both terminals. Counters sell local SIM cards, data packages, and travel adapters.

Medical First Aid: There will be help if you need medical services.

## Shanghai Pudong Airport Overnight Layover

![Shanghai Pudong Airport Rest Area](https://ak-d.tripcdn.com/images/1mk13224x8xqyq2f974D4_C_760_506_R5.jpg?proc=source%2ftrip&proc=source/trip)

### 1. Resting in the Terminal

- Terminal 2 (T2): Mainly for international flights, relatively quiet at night, some seating areas suitable for a short sleep.
- Terminal 1 (T1): Mainly domestic flights; fewer resting areas and busier at night.
- Opening hours: 24/7 access, but shops and restaurants mostly close overnight.

### 2. Pay-per-use Rest Pods / Nap Zones

- Location: Terminal 2, in the international departure area. Look for Nap Zones or capsule lounges.
- Price: Approximately ¥100–¥200 for 3 hours (~S$19–S$38); some charge by the hour.
- Facilities: Lie-flat pods, power outlets, free Wi-Fi; some have small tables for work.
- Opening hours: Often open 24 hours, but booking via the airport app or website in advance is recommended.

## FAQs About Shanghai Pudong Airport

- How far is Shanghai Pudong Airport from the city centre?

 Shanghai Pudong Airport is approximately 30 km east of central Shanghai. A taxi takes around 45–60 minutes, while the Metro Line 2 or Maglev + Metro combination takes 35–75 minutes.
- How early should I arrive for an international flight?

 It’s recommended to arrive at least 3 hours before departure for international flights, due to long check-in, security, and immigration queues. Domestic flights usually require 2 hours.
- Can I use public transport to get to Pudong airport late at night?

 Metro Line 2 and Maglev run approximately 06:00–22:30, so late arrivals may need to use taxis or ride-hailing apps (DiDi). Official airport taxis are available 24/7.
- Are there facilities for families and children at Shanghai Pudong Airport?

 Both terminals have family restrooms, baby changing stations, and some children’s play areas. Lounges often have family-friendly seating.
- Can I access Pudong Airport lounges if I fly economy?

 Yes. Many lounges, such as Plaza Premium Lounge or Aspire Lounge, offer pay-per-use entry for economy passengers, usually around ¥150–¥300 (~S$28–S$57).
