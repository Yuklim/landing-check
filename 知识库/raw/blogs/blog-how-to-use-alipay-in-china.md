## **What is Alipay? (支付宝是啥?)**

![Hand holding a smartphone showing the Alipay app home screen with scan, pay and transfer options](https://www.you.co/sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2024/03/alipay-cny-1.jpg)

[Alipay (支付宝)](https://global.alipay.com/) is China’s leading third-party mobile and online payment platform, widely used for everyday transactions — from buying your morning coffee to train tickets and late-night bubble tea runs. Owned by Ant Group (an affiliate of Alibaba), Alipay has grown from an e-commerce solution into a full digital lifestyle platform with over a billion users globally.

If you’re heading to China from Singapore (or anywhere else), chances are you’ll need Alipay to pay for *almost everything*— food, public transport, shopping, and even tips at your hostel.

The good news? Foreigners can now use Alipay without needing a Chinese bank account — simply link your YouTrip card for seamless payments and competitive CNY exchange rates.

## **Alipay Features**

Alipay boasts a myriad of features designed to streamline financial transactions and your experience in China:

1. **Mobile Payments:** Pay seamlessly using the Alipay app on your smartphone — no more physical cash!
2. **QR Code Payments:**Pay by scanning the merchant QR code or allow merchants to scan your unique payment QR code.
3. **Money Transfers:** While primarily for domestic use, you can receive funds from others within the Alipay ecosystem. Direct international money transfers from Alipay for foreigners are limited, though.
4. Bill Payments: Settle various bills directly from your digital wallet, including:
  - Utility bills
  - Internet services
  - Credit card payments
  - Various other expenses (rent, insurance premiums, subscription services, etc).
  - Note that some may require a Chinese bank account.

5. Public Transport: Pay for buses and subways in over 50 Chinese cities, including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Hangzhou, by scanning QR codes within the app’s ‘Transport’ section.
  - Alipay is also integrated with China’s official train ticket booking system (12306) and supports ride-hailing services.

## **Alipay Tour Card**

![Alipay Tour Pass screen showing a Bank of Shanghai prepaid card with a 100 CNY balance and top-up amounts](https://www.you.co/sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2024/03/alipay-cny-2.jpg)

*Image Credits: The Shutterwhale*

If linking your direct credit/debit card doesn’t work, try the **Alipay Tour card**. The Alipay Tourcard is a **convenient alternative for foreigners**. It’s essentially a **90-day prepaid card** issued by the Bank of Shanghai via Alipay.

This is different from linking your YouTrip card directly to Alipay, as it requires an additional step of loading money before usage.

### **How to Use the Alipay Tour Card:**

1. In Alipay, switch to the International Version
2. Search for ‘**TOURCARD**‘
3. Open the Tourcard mini-app
4. Tap **‘Enter’** > **verify your personal information**
5. Top up your desired amount (Add your YouTrip card to top up)
6. Follow the in-app instructions to complete the top-up

### **Alipay Tour Card: **Limits & Fees****

- Accepted cards: Visa, Mastercard (including YouTrip!), JCB, and Diners Club.
- Maximum top-up limit: 10,000 CNY (~S$1,898)
- Top-up fee: 5% fee on top-up amount
- Valid for 90 days from activation.

Making payments with Alipay is straightforward:

1. **Scan the merchant’s QR code:** Tap the **‘Scan’** button on your ‘Home’ tab and scan the QR code displayed by the merchant.
2. **Allow merchants to scan your QR code:**Tap**‘Pay’** to generate your unique payment QR code for the merchant to scan.

## **Alipay vs WeChat Pay: Which to Use in China?**

Both Alipay and WeChat Pay are dominant mobile payment platforms in China, and it’s highly recommended to have both for comprehensive coverage. However, they have slightly different focuses:

- **Alipay:**Often seen as more focused on financial services, e-commerce (especially for Alibaba-related platforms like Taobao and Tmall), and utilities. 

Its English interface is generally considered more robust and user-friendly for foreigners.
- **WeChat Pay:**Integrated with the WeChat social media app, it’s often preferred for daily expenses, person-to-person transfers, and social interactions. 

Many people already have WeChat installed, making it a natural choice for quick payments.
