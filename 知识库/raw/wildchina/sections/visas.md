# Tourist Visas for China Travel

Most foreign visitors to China will need a tourist visa, also known as an ‘L visa.’ We recommend applying for your tourist visa around 60 days before departure. While we cannot obtain a Chinese visa on your behalf, we are here to guide you through the process and answer any questions you may have. We will also provide the necessary invitation letter for your visa application.

If you plan to enter China just once, a single-entry visa will be sufficient. However, if you plan to leave mainland China (even for Taiwan or Hong Kong) and re-enter, you will need a multiple-entry visa.

While a visa is typically required, it is worth noting that China offers visa-free entry to citizens of select countries for varying durations (commonly between 15-30 days). However, we always recommend enquiring with your country’s Chinese Embassy to confirm whether or not you’ll need a visa.
