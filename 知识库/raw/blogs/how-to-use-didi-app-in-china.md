### Which DiDi App Version is Best for Foreigners Traveling in China?

**For the best experience in mainland China, foreign visitors should use the domestic “DiDi Chuxing” app rather than the international “DiDi Rider” version.** The domestic app provides the most comprehensive vehicle coverage, a full English interface, and better integration with **Alipay and WeChat Pay**, making it a one-stop solution for all your ride-hailing needs.

#### Comparing DiDi Chuxing (Domestic) vs. DiDi Rider (International)

Understanding the difference between these two versions is crucial for a smooth trip.

| **Feature** | **DiDi Chuxing (Domestic) – Recommended** | **DiDi International (DiDi Rider)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Use** | Specifically for mainland China. | Selected overseas markets (e.g., Japan, Australia). |
| **App Scope** | A multifunctional lifestyle platform. | A focused, ride-hailing-only app. |
| **Interface** | Feature-rich, English-supported interface. | Minimalist, international design style. |
| **Payment** | **Alipay, WeChat Pay,** and some international cards. | Mainly international cards and cash. |
| **Vehicle Types** | All options (Express, Premium, Taxi, Luxe). | Limited service types. |

#### Why the Domestic DiDi App is the Superior Choice

- **Comprehensive Service:** It offers the most mature network and the widest range of vehicle options across all Chinese cities.
- **Seamless Payments:** While you can link cards directly, using **Alipay or WeChat Pay** (pre-linked to your international card) within the app provides the highest success rate.
- **Built-in Translation:** The app features real-time message translation, allowing you to text your driver in English while they receive it in Chinese.
- **Intuitive English Mode:** Despite its many features, the English interface is user-friendly and operates similarly to Uber.

> **⚠️ Critical Warning:** When searching the App Store, look for **“DiDi – Didi Chuxing”** or **“滴滴出行”** (usually an orange icon). **Do not** download “滴滴车主” (the green-colored driver app), as that is strictly for people who want to work as DiDi drivers.

### How Do I Register for a DiDi Account with an International Phone Number?

To register for DiDi, open the app and enter your mobile number after selecting your specific country code (e.g., +1 for USA, +44 for UK) from the dropdown menu. DiDi will send a 6-digit SMS verification code to your phone; enter this code to complete your registration. You can then use an international number from over 60 countries to book rides across mainland China.

#### 📝 Step-by-Step Registration Guide

Getting your account ready before you leave the hotel is the best way to ensure a stress-free journey. Follow these steps:

1. **Open the App:** Look for the orange DiDi logo on a white background.
2. **Access the Sign-Up Page:** New users are usually taken here automatically. If not, tap **“Me”** (the person icon) → **“Log in / Sign up.”**
3. **Select Your Country Code (Crucial!):** Tap the dropdown to the left of the phone number field (it defaults to China +86). Search for your home country to apply the correct code.
4. **Verify & Log In:** Tap **“Next,”** receive your 6-digit SMS code, and enter it to log in.

### How do I change the DiDi app language to English?

To switch DiDi to English, tap the **“Me”** icon (bottom right), select the **“Settings”** gear icon (top right), find the **“Language”** option, and select **“English.”** The entire interface will then refresh into English, making navigation as simple as using any western ride-sharing app.

Payments are typically processed automatically via “Password-Free” deduction once the ride ends.

## How to Book a DiDi Ride

To book a DiDi ride, open the app and confirm your pickup location, then enter your destination in English or Pinyin and choose a car type like **Express** or **Premium**. Once you tap the green “Call DiDi” button, wait for a driver to accept the request and always verify their license plate number before entering the vehicle. Payment is processed automatically via your linked Alipay or WeChat Pay account upon arrival.

### How do I set up my trip and enter a destination in the DiDi app?

**To set up your trip, open the DiDi app and ensure the blue GPS pin accurately marks your pickup location; you can long-press and drag the map to adjust it manually.** Tap the **“Where to?”** field to enter your destination using English names, Pinyin, or by pasting a Chinese address for maximum accuracy.

- **Pickup Accuracy:** Don’t just rely on GPS. If you are at a large hotel or metro station, manually pin the specific entrance (e.g., “Exit A”).
- Input Tips:
  - **English:** “Beijing Railway Station” or “The [Forbidden City](https://letstraveltochina.com/the-forbidden-city-travel-guide/).
  - **Pinyin:** Typing “wangfujing” will find 王府井.
  - **Copy-Paste:** Always use the Chinese address provided by your hotel for the most reliable results.

### What are the differences between DiDi car types like Express, Premium, and Taxi?

**DiDi Express is the most economical and commonly used option, similar to UberX, and is the best choice for first-time users.** **DiDi Premium** offers higher-end vehicles and superior service for a slightly higher fare, while **DiDi Taxi** allows you to book official metered cabs through the app interface.

| Ride Type (English) | Key Features | Best For | Price Level (Compared to Express) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discount** | Lowest price; may involve carpooling with other passengers or a short walk to the pickup point; not assigned in real time and requires waiting for system matching. | Solo travelers who are not in a hurry and want the lowest possible fare. | **Lowest** (about 70–80% of Express) |
| **Express** | The standard and most commonly used option; regular private cars; real-time dispatch with fast response. | Most everyday trips, such as getting between city attractions or going to restaurants. | **Baseline price** |
| **Premium** | Upgraded comfort; higher requirements for vehicle age, car model, and driver service; better overall ride experience. | Travelers who value comfort or are on business trips. | **Higher** (about 10–20% more than Express) |
| **Deluxe** | High-end service; typically mid- to high-end sedans (e.g. Audi A6, Mercedes-Benz E-Class); professionally dressed drivers, often with bottled water provided. | Important business meetings, family or group travel, or those seeking a premium experience. | **High** (around 50% more than Express or higher) |
| **Taxi** | Licensed street taxis booked through DiDi; charged by the meter; fares may vary depending on traffic conditions. | Those who prefer traditional taxis or are traveling to destinations with complex routes (narrow streets, alleys) where taxi drivers may be more familiar with local roads. | **Similar to or slightly higher than Express** (metered) |

### How do I confirm my request and identify my DiDi driver?

**After selecting your car type, check the estimated fare range and tap the green “Call DiDi” button to request your ride.** Once a driver accepts, the app will display their **license plate number, car model, color, and rating.** Always verify that the license plate on the physical vehicle matches the app exactly before getting in.

- **Communication:** Use the in-app chat to message your driver; your English will be automatically translated into Chinese.
- **Local Tip:** I usually send a quick message like, *“I am at the pickup location. I am wearing a red jacket.”* It helps the driver spot you in a crowd instantly.

### What should I do for payment during a DiDi ride?

Most rides are **automatically charged** to your linked WeChat Pay, Alipay, or credit card upon arrival, allowing you to simply exit the vehicle once you reach your destination.

- **Automatic Payment:** If your digital wallet is set up, there is no need to swipe a card or handle cash at the end of the trip.
- **Rating:** Leave a 5-star rating for good service. As a local, I can tell you this really helps drivers get better assignments!

## Part 1: DiDi Setup & Registration FAQ

### 1. Do I need WeChat to use DiDi in China?

**No, DiDi is a completely independent app.** While you can use the DiDi “mini-program” inside WeChat, the standalone app is better for travelers as it offers a full English interface. You can download, register, and book rides using just your international phone number and a linked credit card.

- **Pro Tip:** While not required, using **WeChat Pay or Alipay** *within* the DiDi app is the most reliable payment choice for foreign visitors to avoid bank declines.

### 2. Which DiDi app version do I need to download?

**You must download the domestic version, “DiDi Chuxing.”** The international version (DiDi Rider) is designed for overseas markets like Australia or Japan and will not work inside mainland China.

### 3. Do I need a Chinese phone number to use DiDi?

**Not necessarily, but you must have a number that can receive international SMS messages.** You can register using your home phone number; just be sure to manually select your country code (e.g., +1 for USA, +44 for UK) from the dropdown menu during sign-up.

## Part 2: Payments & Costs FAQ

### 4. Does DiDi accept foreign credit cards?

**Yes, but linking them directly can be hit-or-miss.** While DiDi allows direct linking of Visa or Mastercard in major cities like Beijing and Shanghai, these transactions are often blocked by home banks. The **most reliable method** is to link your card to **Alipay or WeChat Pay** first, then select that wallet as your payment method in DiDi.

### 5. Is it expensive to use DiDi in cities like Shanghai?

**DiDi is very affordable compared to Western ride-sharing apps.** Short city trips (3–5 km) typically cost **RMB 20–35**, while a long trip from Pudong Airport to the city center ranges from **RMB 150–200**. Most sightseeing rides within the city will stay under **RMB 80**.

| **Trip Type** | **Estimated Distance** | **Typical Fare (RMB)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Short** | 3–5 km | ¥20 – ¥35 |
| **Medium** | 10 km | ¥35 – ¥60 |
| **Long (Airport)** | 40+ km | ¥150 – ¥200 |

### 6. Do I need to tip my DiDi driver?

**No. Tipping is not expected or customary in China.** The fare shown in the app is the final price. If you received excellent service, the best way to show appreciation is by leaving a **five-star rating** and a positive comment, which helps the driver get better future assignments.

### 8. Why does the driver ask for the last digits of my phone number?

**This is a standard safety verification feature.** The driver sees the last four digits of your registered phone number in their app. They ask for these digits to ensure you are the correct passenger before starting the trip. Please cooperate—it’s for your safety!

### 9. How can I avoid DiDi drivers who smoke?

**To ensure a smoke-free ride, choose “Premium” or “Luxe” car types, as they have much stricter hygiene standards.** You can also add a note saying “No smoking, please” in the remarks section before confirming your booking.

## Part 4: Common Concerns FAQ

### 10. Will DiDi overcharge me?

**Generally, no.** DiDi uses a transparent, GPS-based pricing system. You see an estimate before booking and a detailed digital receipt after. The only variable is **surge pricing** during heavy rain or rush hour, but the app will always ask for your confirmation before you book at a higher rate.

### 11. Can I get a DiDi late at night?

**Yes, car availability remains excellent late at night in major city centers and transport hubs.** However, in remote residential areas, wait times may increase to 10–15 minutes. For maximum safety and comfort at night, I recommend choosing **Premium** or **Luxe** rides.

### 12. How far are DiDi drivers willing to go?

**Most drivers are happy to take you on intercity trips (e.g., Shanghai to Suzhou).** However, for extremely long trips (over 100 km), it’s polite to message the driver after they accept to confirm they are comfortable with the long return journey.
