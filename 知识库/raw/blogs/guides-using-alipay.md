## Why Alipay? What Foreigners Need to Know

Alipay (支付宝) is one of China's two dominant mobile payment platforms, accepted at over one billion merchants — from 5-star restaurants to street food stalls, metro stations, pharmacies, hospitals, and online shopping. For foreigners visiting China in 2026, Alipay is the easiest option to set up because it accepts foreign Visa and Mastercard directly, with no Chinese bank account needed.

The key change in recent years: Alipay no longer requires a complicated "Tour Pass" workaround for most travelers. You can now bind your foreign card directly to your Alipay account, just like a Chinese resident would. This guide covers the current direct-binding method, which is faster, cheaper, and more reliable.

## Before You Start: What You Need

### Required Items

- **A valid, unexpired passport** — this is your identity document for eKYC. Expired passports are not accepted.
- **A foreign phone number** — any number with a country code (+1, +44, +65, +60, etc.). Used for registration and OTP codes.
- **A supported physical card** — Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Discover, or Diners Club. Virtual cards and most prepaid cards are rejected (see card section below).
- **A stable internet connection** — Wi-Fi is preferred for the video-based face recognition step.

### Before You Travel: Two Important Steps

⚠️ Call your bank before you arrive in China

Many banks automatically block the first Alipay charge as suspected fraud — because it looks like an unfamiliar overseas e-commerce transaction. A quick call to your bank's international line to say "I'll be using my card for mobile payments in China" can prevent this.

Also ask them to confirm the **exact name format** they have on file for your card (first name, middle initial, last name order). This must match your Alipay registration exactly.

🚫 Disable your VPN before opening Alipay

Alipay's fraud detection actively flags VPN connections. If you're connected to a VPN during registration or card binding, your verification will fail or your account may be temporarily blocked. Turn off VPN, then open the app.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Alipay as a Foreigner

### Step 1: Download Alipay

Download the Alipay app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). There is only one Alipay app — when you register with a foreign phone number, it will automatically configure itself for international use (English interface, foreign card support). You do not need a separate "international version" app.

### Step 2: Register with Your Foreign Phone Number

1. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**
2. Select your country code and enter your foreign phone number
3. Enter the OTP verification code sent to your phone
4. Set a login password (6+ characters)
5. Accept the terms and complete basic account creation

### Step 3: Complete eKYC Identity Verification

This is required to unlock full payment features and higher transaction limits. The process involves two parts:

1. **Passport scan:** Tap on your profile → Identity verification. Scan your passport data page (the photo page). The app will automatically read your name, passport number, date of birth, and expiry date. Review and confirm these details are correct.
2. **Face recognition:** Complete a liveness check — you'll be asked to blink, turn your head, or smile. This matches your face to your passport photo. Do this in good lighting with your face clearly visible.

Verification typically completes within a few hours, but can take up to 3 business days. During this period, your account works but with lower spending limits. Once verified, Alipay sends a notification to confirm.

### Step 4: Add Your Foreign Card

1. Go to Me → Bank Cards → Add Card
2. Enter your card number, expiry date, and CVV
3. Enter the cardholder name **exactly as it appears on your card**
4. Complete card verification — Alipay may send a small test charge to your card (typically refunded immediately) to confirm ownership
5. Your bank may send an OTP to your registered phone or email for authorization

### Step 5: Make a Small Test Payment

Before relying on Alipay in a busy situation (like at a restaurant or taxi), test it with a small ¥1 payment somewhere. Many convenience stores and pharmacy chains accept Alipay. A successful test payment confirms your card is active and your account is fully functional.

### Which Specific Cards Work Best (Community Reports)

Based on reports from Reddit, Xiaohongshu, and expat forums in 2025–2026:

- **Wise physical card (Mastercard)** — widely reported as working well. Note: use the physical card, not a virtual number.
- **Revolut physical card (Visa/Mastercard)** — physical card often works; virtual card is usually blocked.
- **HSBC international cards** — consistently reported as reliable across multiple markets.
- **Standard Chartered, DBS, OCBC** (Singapore) — generally good compatibility.
- **Citibank global cards** — usually works, though some users report initial bank blocks.
- **US-issued cards** — mixed results; Citi and Chase Visa tend to work more reliably than smaller US banks. Your bank's fraud settings are usually the deciding factor, not Alipay.

## Transaction Limits & Fees for Foreign Cards

Limits are set by the People's Bank of China (PBOC) and depend on whether you've completed passport (Real-Name) verification. The tier below was raised by PBOC in March 2024 and remains current in 2026.

| Account Status | Single Transaction | Annual Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| Before passport verification | ~$500 USD | ~$2,000 USD |
| After passport verification (recommended) | $5,000 USD (~¥36,000 RMB) | $50,000 USD (~¥360,000 RMB) |

Note: PBOC defines single-transaction and annual limits only; there is no official monthly cap. Converted RMB figures are approximate (rate ~¥7.2 = $1 USD).

| Transaction Amount | Service Fee |
|---|---|
| Under ¥200 RMB | Free |
| ¥200 and above | 3% of transaction amount |

Note: New users may receive a promotional period (commonly 60 days) where the 3% fee is waived. Check the Alipay app for current promotions. Your home bank may also charge their own foreign transaction fee on top of this.

## How to Pay with Alipay in China

### Method 1: Scan the Merchant's QR Code (Most Common)

1. Open Alipay → tap the **Scan** icon at the top
2. Point your camera at the merchant's QR code (displayed at the counter or on a stand)
3. Enter the amount if prompted (some QR codes are static; others auto-fill the amount)
4. Confirm with your fingerprint, Face ID, or 6-digit payment password
5. You'll see a green checkmark and receive an instant notification

### Method 2: Show Your Payment QR Code

1. Open Alipay → tap **Pay** (or the barcode icon)
2. Show the QR code / barcode on your screen to the cashier
3. They scan it from their end — payment is processed automatically
4. Confirm with fingerprint, Face ID, or payment password

## TourCard: Alipay's Prepaid Option (When to Use It)

If direct card binding fails for your specific card, Alipay offers a secondary option called **TourCard** — a prepaid virtual card issued by the Bank of Shanghai, accessible as an Alipay mini-program.

### When TourCard Makes Sense

- Your foreign card is repeatedly rejected during direct binding
- You want to send P2P money transfers (direct binding doesn't support this)
- You need to pay at merchants that only accept Alipay wallet balance, not linked cards
- You need to buy train or domestic flight tickets through Alipay

### TourCard Considerations

- You load it with money in advance using your foreign card — any unused balance must be refunded before the card expires (refunds can take several weeks)
- There are top-up fees and the card has an expiry date
- For most travelers, **direct binding is simpler and better** — only use TourCard if direct binding doesn't work for you

## Common Issues & Solutions

- **Card issuing bank declined?** Your bank — not Alipay — is blocking the transaction. This is the most common issue for foreigners. See our [complete Alipay issuing bank declined fix guide](/blog/alipay-issuing-bank-declined-fix) for 5 proven solutions including what to say when you call your bank.
- **eKYC verification failed?** Make sure you're in good lighting, your passport is flat and fully visible (no glare), and your face is clearly visible during the liveness check. Try again in a brighter spot. If it keeps failing, try the manual entry option instead of scanning.
- **Virtual or prepaid card rejected?** Use a physical credit or debit card. Revolut physical card (not virtual number) or Wise physical card tend to work.
- **App in Chinese?** Go to Me → Settings → General → Language and select English.
- **Revolut or Wise card?** Use the **physical card**, not a virtual card number. Our [Revolut + Alipay guide](/blog/revolut-alipay-china-guide) covers the setup in detail.
- **Payment fails at a specific merchant?** Some small vendors only accept local Alipay wallet balance, which foreign cards can't fund. Use cash as a backup at small street stalls.
- **Account locked or blocked?** Alipay may temporarily lock accounts that show unusual activity patterns. Contact Alipay support through the app (Me → Help Center) or visit an Alipay service counter at major airports.

## Safety & Security Tips

- Always double-check the merchant's QR code before scanning — fraudulent QR codes are rare but not unheard of
- Set up Face ID or fingerprint authentication for payments — faster and more secure than a PIN
- Never share your payment password with anyone
- Review your transaction history regularly in the Alipay app
- Keep a backup card and some cash for situations where Alipay is not accepted
