# The Fastest Way to Enter China: WildChina’s VIP Airport Arrival Service

Navigating arriving in a new international airport while jet lagged is rarely a highlight experience of any trip, especially with a foreign language added in to the mix.

![The Fastest Way to Enter China: WildChina’s VIP Airport Arrival Service](https://wildchina.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/shutterstock_320573189-1024x683.jpg)

## **What to expect:**

**Personalized greeting right off the plane:** As soon as your plane touches down in China, and you cross the passenger bridge connecting the plane to the airport itself, an English-speaking airport VIP representative will be waiting to meet you, holding a customized sign with your name so you can’t miss them.  (If you’d like a VIP representative proficient in a language other than English or Mandarin, please let us know and we will check availability on your behalf.)

**Smooth customs process**: As almost all international arrivals to China will need a visa, the customs process is done mostly manually, with a Chinese customs official checking all passports and visas for new arrivals. Your VIP arrival service representative will escort you from your arrival gate to customs and through the entire customs process, including the fingerprint collection and document check.

**Fast-Track Access:**VIP guests have access to a special fast-pass lane which bypasses the main queue. This means less waiting and more time to relax in your awaiting hotel room! Please note that fast-track access is not available for international travelers holding 144-hour free transit visas. This service only applies to those with a Chinese tourist visa (L visa).

**Baggage Claim Luggage Assistance:**After immigration come the baggage claim carousels. Your airport VIP representative will guide to the correct carousel andtake care of collecting your baggage as you indicate. They will then transport your baggage for you (no need to worry about lugging it yourself!) through the customs checks.

**Seamless Transition:**Your airport VIP representative willescort you and your luggage through the post-customs doors into the arrivals hall, where your WildChina guide will be waiting to meet you and your chauffeur will be on standby outside, ready to whisk you off to your hotel!

### VIP Airport Arrival Service FAQ:

**How much does it cost?**

The booking fee for WildChina’s VIP airport arrival service starts from around $250USD per person. Pricing varies depending on location though, so please get in touch for a specific quote for your trip.

**Is there a departure service as well?**

Yes, the same fast-track service is available for departures (though it does not include check-in). Get in touch with us for details.

**Does fast-track access apply to travelers with 144-hour transit visas?**

No, fast-track access is not available for travelers holding a 144-hour free transit visa. This service is only available for those with a Chinese tourist visa (L visa).

144-hour visa holders have dedicated counters for check-in and border inspection, though these do not offer fast-track privileges.

**Which airports is this service available at?**

The WildChina VIP airport arrival service is available at most international airports around China including Shanghai (PVG & SHA), Beijing (PEK & PKX), Xi’an (SIA), Hong Kong (HKG), Guangzhou (CAN), Qingdao (TAO), and Chengdu (CTU) international airports. [Get in touch with us](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#731a1d151c33041a1f17101b1a1d125d101c1e) to find out more or to inquire about specific airports not listed above.

**Is this service available for cruise port arrivals as well?**

This service is not currently available at China’s international cruise ports.

**Other than English, what languages are available?**

VIP Airport arrival service is currently available in English and Chinese. For other languages, [get in touch with us](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#375e59515877405e5b53545f5e59561954585a) to inquire.
