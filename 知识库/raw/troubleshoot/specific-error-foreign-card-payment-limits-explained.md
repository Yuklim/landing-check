# Alipay & WeChat Pay Limits for Foreigners: Full Breakdown (2026)

# Alipay & WeChat Pay: Foreign Card Payment Limits

For a complete overview of all payment options in China, please read our [Ultimate Guide to Paying in China](/).

## Why It Happens

For foreign users, there are official limits on how much you can pay with your international card through these apps.

- **Platform Rules:** Alipay and WeChat have set limits for foreign cards based on regulations.
- **Your Own Bank's Limits:** Your personal credit card has its own daily spending limit.
- **Merchant Restrictions:** Some small shops or individual sellers cannot accept payments funded by foreign cards.

## Understanding the Limits (Updated 2026)

These are the official limits set by the platforms for foreign card users. *Note: These can change.*

| Limit Type | Approximate Amount (CNY) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single Transaction Limit | ~ ¥6,000 - ¥7,000 | The maximum for one single payment. |
| Annual Cumulative Limit | ~ ¥70,000 | The total amount you can spend in one year. |

## How to Manage These Limits

1. Solution for Large Payments: Split the TransactionIf you need to buy something expensive (e.g., an item for ¥10,000), you cannot pay for it in one transaction.**What to do:** Ask the shopkeeper if you can pay in two parts. For example, make two separate payments of ¥5,000 each. This is a very common practice.
2. Solution for Merchant RestrictionsIf your payment fails at a small shop or a street vendor, it's likely they cannot accept foreign card payments. This is not your fault.**What to do:** Try to pay at a larger, more established store like a major supermarket, restaurant chain, or department store. They are more likely to accept it.
3. Can I Increase These Limits?Yes — and you don't need to be a resident. Tourists who complete passport (real-name) verification in the app unlock the higher tier: roughly **$5,000 USD per single transaction** and **$50,000 USD per year** (limits the People's Bank of China raised in March 2024). Until you verify, you're capped at a no-verification allowance of about **$2,000 USD total**. Verification usually takes only a few minutes, so it's worth doing before a big purchase.
