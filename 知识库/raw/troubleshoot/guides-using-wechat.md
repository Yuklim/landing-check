## Why Use WeChat Pay in China?

WeChat Pay (微信支付, also called Weixin Pay) is accepted at over 80 million merchants across China — restaurants, shops, supermarkets, taxis, hospitals, museums, and online platforms. For foreigners visiting China in 2026, WeChat Pay is one of two essential mobile payment methods (alongside Alipay). Setting up both gives you the best coverage.

Since 2023, WeChat Pay has significantly expanded support for foreign cards, allowing foreigners to pay directly using an international credit card linked to their WeChat account — no Chinese bank account required. However, some features remain limited for foreign cardholders (covered below).

## Before You Start: What You Need

### Required Items

- **A valid, unexpired passport** — required for identity verification (eKYC). Expired documents are not accepted.
- **A foreign phone number** — any international number (+1, +44, +65, +60, etc.) for registration and OTP codes.
- **A foreign card** (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, or Discover) — both credit and debit cards are supported. Prepaid cards and virtual card numbers are generally not accepted.
- **Stable internet connection** — Wi-Fi preferred for the face recognition step.

⚠️ Call your bank before arriving in China

WeChat Pay charges appear as small online transactions from a Chinese payment processor. Many banks' fraud systems automatically block these. A quick call to your bank to say "I'll be making small e-commerce payments in China via WeChat Pay" can prevent failed transactions.

Also confirm the **exact name format** on your card — it must match your WeChat profile and passport exactly (including name order and any middle names or initials).

🚫 Name mismatch is the #1 cause of card rejection

The name you enter in WeChat must match both your passport **and** your bank card exactly. JOHN SMITH is different from John Smith. J. Allen Smith is different from John Allen Smith. When in doubt, call your bank to confirm the exact name on record.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up WeChat Pay as a Foreigner

### Step 1: Download WeChat

Download WeChat from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). There is one WeChat app globally — you do not need a special international version. Register with your foreign phone number.

### Step 2: Register Your Account

1. Open WeChat → tap **Sign Up**
2. Enter your name, foreign phone number, and country code
3. Enter the OTP sent to your phone
4. Set a password and complete the security prompts
5. Note: New WeChat accounts may need an existing WeChat user to "verify" them — ask a friend or find a verification helper online if prompted

### Step 3: Enable the Wallet Feature

If you don't see a "Wallet" option in WeChat, you need to enable it manually:

1. Go to Me → Settings → General → Features
2. Find **Wallet** in the features list and toggle it on
3. Return to Me → Services — you should now see **Wallet**

### Step 4: Verify Your Identity (eKYC)

1. Go to Me → Services → Wallet
2. Tap **Cards** → **Add Card**
3. WeChat will prompt you for identity verification before you can add a card
4. Submit a clear photo of your passport's data page (the photo page)
5. Complete a selfie / liveness check — blink or move your head as prompted
6. Enter your details and submit for review

### Step 5: Add Your Foreign Credit Card

1. After identity verification, return to Wallet → Cards → Add a Card
2. Enter your card number, expiry date, CVV, and billing address
3. Enter the cardholder name **exactly as it appears on your card**
4. Authorize the card — WeChat or your bank may send an OTP for confirmation
5. Your card is now linked. It will appear in your WeChat Wallet.

### Step 6: Make a Test Payment

Before using WeChat Pay in a real situation, test it with a small payment — a convenience store or pharmacy is a good low-pressure option. A successful ¥1–5 test payment confirms your card and account are fully functional.

## Which Cards Work with WeChat Pay?

### Officially Supported Card Networks

| Card Network | Supported? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visa (credit) | ✅ Yes | Best compatibility |
| Mastercard (credit) | ✅ Yes | Best compatibility |
| JCB (credit) | ✅ Yes | Good option for Japanese cardholders |
| Discover / Diners Club | ✅ Yes | Supported |
| American Express | ⚠️ Limited | Some sources indicate support; test before relying on it |
| Foreign debit cards (Visa/MC/JCB) | ✅ Yes | Both credit and debit cards supported |
| Prepaid / virtual cards | ❌ Not supported | Physical credit card required |

### Which Specific Cards Tend to Work (Community Reports)

- **HSBC international credit cards** — consistently reported as reliable across markets (UK, HK, SG, etc.)
- **Citibank Visa/Mastercard** — generally good compatibility
- **Standard Chartered, DBS, OCBC** (Singapore) — good results reported by Singapore users
- **Chase Sapphire, Citi Premier** (US) — often works; call Chase/Citi international line first to whitelist China payments
- **Barclays, Lloyds, NatWest** (UK) — mixed results; UK banks are more likely to block initial charges as fraud

|---|---|

Offers change, so check the WeChat Pay section in your app for the current promotion. Your home bank may charge its own foreign transaction fee separately.

## How to Pay with WeChat Pay in China

### Method 1: Show Your Payment QR Code (Most Common)

1. Open WeChat → Me → Services → Wallet → Money → Receive/Pay
2. Show your QR code or barcode to the cashier
3. They scan it from their terminal — the amount is deducted from your linked card
4. Confirm with Face ID, fingerprint, or your 6-digit payment PIN
5. You'll get an instant notification with the transaction details

### Method 2: Scan the Merchant's QR Code

1. Open WeChat → tap the **Scan** icon (top right of the main screen)
2. Point your camera at the merchant's QR code
3. Enter the amount if it's a static QR code, or confirm the pre-filled amount
4. Authorize with Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN

## Important Limitations for Foreign Cardholders

Foreign cards linked to WeChat Pay work for most everyday purchases, but there are meaningful restrictions you should know before relying on it:

| Feature | Available with Foreign Card? |
|---|---|
| Pay at physical merchants (QR code) | ✅ Yes |
| Pay in WeChat mini-programs / apps | ✅ Yes |
| Send money to friends (P2P transfers) | ❌ Not available |
| Send / receive red packets (红包) | ❌ Not available |
| Top up WeChat wallet balance | ❌ Not available (Chinese bank cards only) |
| Book train / flight tickets via WeChat | ⚠️ Limited — depends on the mini-program |

If you need P2P transfers or red packets, you'll need a Chinese bank account. For most tourists, the merchant payment functionality is sufficient for day-to-day needs.

## Common Issues & Solutions

- **Card declined at payment?** See our detailed [WeChat Pay card declined fix guide](/blog/wechat-pay-card-declined-fix) — covers name mismatch, soft reset (unbind and re-add your card), the ¥0.01 micro-donation "icebreaker" trick, and how to call China Construction Bank's support line.
- **Card keeps getting rejected during setup?** Make sure you're using a physical card (not a virtual card number), and that the name matches your passport exactly. Prepaid cards are not accepted. If your physical Visa/Mastercard is still failing, try Alipay as a backup — see our [Alipay setup guide](/guides/using-alipay).
- **Wallet not visible in the app?** Go to Me → Settings → General → Features and enable the Wallet feature.
- **Can't pass identity verification?** Make sure you're in good lighting, your passport is flat with no glare on the photo page, and your selfie lighting is clear. Try again in a brighter environment. If it keeps failing after multiple attempts, try the manual entry option.
- **Account asks for a "helper" verification?** New WeChat accounts sometimes require an existing user to verify you. Ask a friend, hotel staff, or look for a verification helper in expat Facebook groups for your destination city.
- **Using Revolut or Wise?** Remember: WeChat Pay only supports credit cards. Revolut and Wise issue Mastercard/Visa cards — but check whether yours is classified as credit or debit. Our [Revolut + Alipay guide](/blog/revolut-alipay-china-guide) has fintech card tips for China.
- **Payment failed at a specific merchant?** Some small vendors only accept WeChat wallet balance (which foreign cards can't fund) or specific payment types. Cash remains a useful backup at small vendors.

## Safety & Security Tips

- Enable Face ID or fingerprint authentication for WeChat Pay — required for payment authorization and provides a layer of protection if your phone is stolen
- Always verify the merchant's QR code before scanning — fraudulent QR codes redirect payments to scammers
- Never share your WeChat payment PIN with anyone
- If your phone is lost or stolen, immediately freeze your WeChat Pay via another device (log in → Me → Settings → Account Security)
- Keep a backup card and some cash — not every merchant accepts WeChat Pay, and having alternatives prevents being stuck
