# Can Foreigners Use DiDi in China? (2026 Guide)

Verified · Last verified 2026-06-29

**Yes.** Foreign visitors can use **DiDi** (China’s main ride-hail app) through the standalone app, **WeChat**, or **Alipay** — verified against the **Shanghai government English guide** and **Alipay+** transport documentation.

We have not completed a full ride booking in 2026. Fares, surge pricing, and UI labels change — follow what your app shows.

## Short answer

DiDi supports **English** and **international phone numbers** in the standalone app (per Shanghai.gov). You still need **mobile data** and a working **Alipay or WeChat Pay** wallet for most booking paths. For step-by-step airport pickup and payment edge cases, see our full [DiDi transport guide](/transport/didi-china/).

## Three ways foreigners open DiDi

- **DiDi app** — download “DiDi” from your app store; register with an international number; switch language under Me → Settings → General → Language → English.
- **WeChat mini program** — Services → Ride Hailing, or search “DiDi”; fare deducts from WeChat Pay.
- **Alipay** — search “DiDi” on the home screen; fare deducts from Alipay balance (Shanghai government guide).

## First-ride checklist

1. Set up **Alipay and/or WeChat Pay** with your passport and foreign card — see [how to pay in China](/money/pay-in-china-foreigners/).
2. Install **Amap (Gaode)** or save your hotel address in Chinese — [maps guide](/internet/google-maps-china/).
3. Open DiDi via your chosen path and confirm **English UI**.
4. Enter destination → pick car tier → wait for driver match.
5. Verify **license plate** and car model before entering; fare usually auto-deducts at trip end.

## What you need — and common blockers

Works for typical tourist rides **within mainland China** when mobile pay is active and you have data (eSIM or local SIM). In-app chat can auto-translate messages to drivers (Shanghai.gov).

Mini programs pay only from **that wallet** — you cannot mix Alipay and WeChat in the same mini program. Cash is uncommon; if card linking fails, fix wallets first rather than assuming DiDi accepts foreign cards directly.

## On-ride tips

- Airport pickups: follow terminal signs to the designated ride-hail pickup zone.
- Peak hours and rain may trigger **surge pricing** — compare tiers before confirming.
- Screenshot your route if language is a concern.
- Official metered taxis remain a backup if the app fails.

## Common mistakes

- Opening DiDi before Alipay/WeChat Pay works
- Using only Google Maps — drivers expect Chinese addresses or Amap pins
- Getting into a car without checking the license plate in the app
- Assuming the WeChat mini program accepts Alipay (or vice versa)
- Skipping English language setup and struggling with Chinese-only menus
