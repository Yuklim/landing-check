## 🌍 Solution #3: Check WeChat Pay Region Settings[↑ Back to top](#top)

Your [WeChat account](/guides/using-wechat) region settings can affect payment functionality, especially for international users visiting China.

#### 📱 Step-by-Step Region Check

Open [WeChat](/guides/using-wechat)

Go to "Me" → "Settings" → "General" → "Language"

Check Region Setting

Should match your current location in China

Set to "China"

If you're in mainland China

Restart WeChat

Close and reopen the app

### Common Region-Related Issues:

- **Account Created Outside China:** If you created your [WeChat account](/guides/using-wechat) outside China, some payment features may be limited
- **Phone Number Mismatch:** Your registered phone number region should match your current location (related to [SMS verification issues](/tool/specific-error/fix-sms-verification-code-error))
- **Travel Mode:** If you're frequently traveling, WeChat may restrict payment features for security (learn about [payment security in China](/blog/pay-in-china-2025-alipay-refunds))

**Note:** Since 2023, [WeChat Pay](/guides/using-wechat) allows international users to link foreign cards regardless of account region, but some legacy accounts may still have restrictions. For verification issues, see our [SMS verification guide](/tool/specific-error/fix-sms-verification-code-error).

## 📱 Solution #4: Use the International Version (WeChat) vs. the Mainland Version (Weixin)[↑ Back to top](#top)

**2024 Update:** Both WeChat versions now support foreign cards, but with different features. The distinction is still important for full functionality. For complete setup instructions, see our [WeChat Pay guide](/guides/using-wechat).

### Key Differences:

| Feature | WeChat (International) | Weixin (Mainland) |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign Card Support | 🟡 Limited support (restrictions apply) | ✅ Full support for payments |
| Basic Payments | ✅ Supported (with limits) | ✅ Fully supported |
| Red Packets (with foreign cards) | ❌ Not available | ❌ Not available (requires Chinese bank card) |
| Money Transfers (with foreign cards) | ❌ Not available | ❌ Not available (requires Chinese bank card) |
| Wallet Top-up | ❌ Not supported | ❌ Not supported (Chinese cards only) |

### How to Get the Right Version (2024 Update):

- **In China:** Download from Chinese app stores to get Weixin with full foreign card support (see our [WeChat setup guide](/guides/using-wechat))
- **Outside China:** International WeChat now supports foreign cards but with some limitations (see our [complete WeChat guide](/guides/using-wechat))
- **Registration matters:** Chinese phone numbers get Weixin features; international numbers get WeChat features
- **Check your app:** The mainland version shows Chinese characters (微信) in the app store

**2024 Update:** Foreign cards now work on both versions, but Weixin (mainland version) offers better integration and fewer restrictions. For merchants, both versions support basic payments, but advanced features like Red Packets require Chinese bank cards regardless of app version. For more about setting up payments in China, see our [comprehensive payment guide](/).

If the above solutions don't work, contacting WeChat Pay support directly can resolve account-specific issues. You can also check our [payment error codes lookup](/tool/payment-error-codes) for specific error meanings.

**Call: 95017**

Choose 0 → then 9 for English

Me → Settings → Help & Feedback → Contact Us

9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Beijing Time)

For urgent issues, check our [self-help error guide](/tool/payment-error-codes)

### What to Say (Template):

*"Hello, I am a foreign visitor in China trying to link my international credit card to WeChat Pay. I keep getting an 'Unsupported Card for This Service' error. My card is a [Visa/Mastercard/etc.] issued by [Bank Name]. Can you please check if there are any restrictions on my account or if my card type is supported? My WeChat ID is [Your WeChat ID]."*

### Information to Have Ready:

- Your [WeChat ID](/guides/using-wechat) or phone number
- Card type (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
- Issuing bank name
- Country where the card was issued
- Screenshots of the error message (refer to our [error codes reference](/tool/payment-error-codes))
