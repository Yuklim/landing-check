# Alipay Setup Guide 2026: Complete Walkthrough

Total Setup Time: 15-30 minutes

Most users complete setup in under 20 minutes. Card verification may take an additional 24-48 hours, so start at least 3 days before your trip.

## Why Alipay is Essential for China Travel

Alipay (支付宝) is China's most widely accepted mobile payment platform. With over 1.3 billion users, it's the backbone of daily commerce across the country — from high-end malls to street food vendors.

### 95% Acceptance

Accepted at virtually every merchant in China

### Full English UI

Complete English language support for international users

### Tour Pass

Dedicated feature designed specifically for tourists

## What You'll Need

### Before You Start, Gather:

iOS 12+ or Android 8+

Visa, Mastercard, JCB, or Discover

For SMS verification (international numbers work)

Card Compatibility Note

Not all cards work with Alipay. Check with your bank before traveling:

✅ Usually Works:

- • Mastercard debit/credit cards
- • JCB cards
- • Discover cards

❌ Often Rejected:

- • American Express (AMEX)
- • Some prepaid cards
- • Virtual card numbers
- • Some regional bank cards

## Step-by-Step Setup Guide

### Download Alipay

Download the official Alipay app from your device's app store. Make sure you're downloading the genuine app — look for "Alipay" by "Alipay (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd."

### Set Language to English

When you first open Alipay, it may default to Chinese. Change the language immediately:

1. 1.Open Alipay and tap **"Me"** (bottom right corner)
2. 2.Tap the **gear icon ⚙️** (top right) to open Settings
3. 3.Tap **"General"** (通用设置)
4. 4.Tap **"Language"** (多语言)
5. 5.Select **"English"** and confirm

### Register Your Account

Create your Alipay account using your international phone number:

1. 1.Tap **"Sign Up"** on the welcome screen
2. 2.Select your country code (e.g., +1 for USA, +44 for UK)
3. 3.Enter your phone number
4. 4.Tap **"Get Verification Code"**
5. 5.Enter the 6-digit code sent via SMS
6. 6.Create a strong password (8+ characters, mix of letters and numbers)

![Sign up with phone number demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2FiosStep2.gif&w=828&q=75)

**SMS not arriving?** Wait 60 seconds and try again. If it still fails, try using a different phone number or contact Alipay support.

### Access Tour Pass

The Tour Pass is Alipay's special feature for international visitors. It lets you use Alipay without a Chinese bank account.

1. 1.From the home screen, tap the **search bar** at the top
2. 2.Type **"Tour Pass"** and search
3. 3.Tap on the **Tour Pass** mini-program
4. 4.Tap **"Get Started"** to begin setup

**Alternative:**You may also see "Tour Pass" promoted on the home screen if Alipay detects you're an international user.

### Verify Your Identity

You'll need to verify your identity using your passport. This is required by Chinese regulations.

1. 1.Select **"Passport"** as your ID type
2. 2.Enter your **full name** exactly as it appears on your passport
3. 3.Enter your **passport number**
4. 4.Select your **nationality**
5. 5.Take a **photo of your passport** information page
6. 6.Complete **facial verification** (look at camera and follow prompts)

Photo Tips:

- • Ensure good lighting — avoid shadows on passport
- • Hold passport flat and fill the frame
- • Make sure all text is clearly readable
- • Remove passport cover if present

### Add Your Bank Card

Link your international Visa, Mastercard, JCB, or Discover card to fund your Tour Pass.

1. 1.In Tour Pass, tap **"Add Card"**
2. 2.Select your card network (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
3. 3.Enter your **card number**
4. 4.Enter **expiration date** and **CVV**
5. 5.Enter your **billing address**
6. 6.Agree to terms and tap **"Confirm"**

**Bank Notification:** You may receive a fraud alert from your bank. This is normal — approve the transaction to continue. Consider notifying your bank of your China travel plans beforehand.

Visual Guide:

![Add bank card demonstration](/_next/image?url=%2Fgifs%2FiosStep5.gif&w=828&q=75)

### Load Your Tour Pass

Add funds to your Tour Pass balance. This is what you'll spend when making payments in China.

1. 1.Tap **"Top Up"** or **"Add Money"**
2. 2.Enter the amount in CNY (Chinese Yuan)
3. 3.Confirm the transaction
4. 4.Complete any additional bank verification (3D Secure, etc.)

## Making Your First Payment

Once your Tour Pass is loaded, you're ready to pay! There are two ways to pay with Alipay:

### Scan to Pay

You scan the merchant's QR code

1. 1. Open Alipay
2. 2. Tap **"Scan"** (top of screen)
3. 3. Point camera at merchant's QR code
4. 4. Enter amount if prompted
5. 5. Confirm payment

### Show to Pay

Merchant scans your QR code

1. 1. Open Alipay
2. 2. Tap **"Pay"** (shows your QR code)
3. 3. Show code to merchant
4. 4. Merchant scans your code
5. 5. Payment completes automatically

Test Before You Travel

Make a small test transaction at home to confirm everything works. Some suggestions:

- • Top up your Tour Pass with the minimum ¥100
- • Find a local Chinese restaurant that accepts Alipay
- • Use the in-app utilities (phone top-up, etc.)

## Essential Security Settings

Protect your Alipay account with these recommended security settings:

### Enable Payment Password

Set a 6-digit PIN for all payments

Settings → Payment Settings → Payment Password

### Enable Biometrics

Use Face ID or fingerprint for faster, secure payments

Settings → Payment Settings → Biometric Payment

### Set Transaction Limits

Limit max payment amount for added protection

Settings → Payment Settings → Payment Limits

### Enable Login Notifications

Get alerts for account activity

Settings → Security → Login Notifications
