# PKX TRAVELPASS

![](../../../../images/triangle.png)

![](../../../../images/triangle.png)

"TRAVEL PASS" is a product developed jointly by China UnionPay, the Bank of Beijing, and China Unicom, aimed at offering inbound travelers both easy payment and communication services. The product is a portable device that integrates mobile Wi-Fi and QR code payment functions, which can accommodate both a SIM card and a bank card, providing users with a range of services, such as mobile Wi-Fi access, QR code payment, and translation.

**Where to Apply:**

- Beijing Capital International Airport: China Unicom's service hall

- Beijing Daxing International Airport: the "Mobile Services" section of the Airport Service Counter, and China Unicom's service hall

**Pricing:**

The products are sold in a package format:

1.SIM cards for communication are available in four durations: 7 days, 10 days, 15 days, and 30 days.

2.The terminal device is priced at CNY 400. During the trial period, the device can be rented for free, but a deposit of CNY 400 is required.

3.The Bank of Beijing cards can be applied for free.

**Service Scope:**

1.Communication: After inserting your SIM card into the device, you can gain access to several services including Internet, mobile Wi-Fi, and SMS reception. The SIM card can also be used on a mobile phone to enable voice calls.

2.Payment: You can pay by scanning QR codes at China's consumption venues that display the logos of UnionPay and/or Cloud QuickPass. Additionally, you can pay through the device at some consumption venues with green signs showing the payment codes of WeChat.

**Top-up Services:**

1.SIM Card: The card can be topped up at a service hall of China Unicom or via the official App of China Unicom.

2.Bank Card: The card can be topped up by using CNY cash at any of the ATMs of the Bank of Beijing, with a maximum limit of CNY 10,000.

**How to Request Refunds:**

1.SIM Card: The service plan will automatically expire, and the remaining balance is non-refundable.

2.Bank Card: Every cardholder can use the remaining balance for consumption before exiting China or withdraw it through ATMs or the service counters of the Bank of Beijing.

**Where to Return the Device:**

The terminal device can be returned at China Unicom service halls located near the arrivals exits of the following airports:

1.Beijing Capital International Airport (Terminal 3)

2.Beijing Daxing International Airport

Shanghai Pudong International Airport
