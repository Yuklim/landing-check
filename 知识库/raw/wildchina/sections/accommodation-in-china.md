# Accommodation in China

WildChina goes the extra mile to find the best possible accommodations for our clients, based on the available options. Our operations team actively monitors and surveys hotels to ensure quality, often providing additional amenities when possible. When selecting hotels, we consider several factors, including your preferences for style and comfort, the type of accommodation, the availability of English-speaking staff, and the best available options in the area.

Explore our curated selection of the best boutique hotels in China.

 electricity Hotels

China offers a wide range of hotel options. In major cities, many properties rival the world’s top luxury hotels, offering exceptional comfort and service. In more remote provinces, however, accommodation standards can be simpler, with fewer amenities and a more local character. Experiencing this contrast is part of what makes traveling in China so rewarding—it reflects the variety of landscapes, cultures, and ways of life. Kindly note that in some older hotels, heating and cooling may be controlled centrally rather than from your in-room thermostat.

 Rooms

Most hotels in China offer standard room options. If you’re traveling with children, we recommend requesting an extra bed or connecting rooms, as rooms with two beds typically feature twin-sized beds. Be aware that Chinese mattresses tend to be firmer than those in Western countries.

 electricity Laundry

While most luxury hotels offer dry cleaning services, many local or boutique hotels may not. We recommend that guests use laundry services at the main hotels when available. However, for other accommodations or a more cost-effective solution for larger loads, Laundry Town is an excellent alternative.
