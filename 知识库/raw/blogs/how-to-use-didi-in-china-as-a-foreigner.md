# How to Use DiDi in China as a Foreigner: Complete 2026 Guide

Getting around China can be challenging for first-time visitors, especially when there is a language barrier. While traditional taxis are still available, most travelers now use DiDi Chuxing (滴滴出行), China’s largest ride-hailing app, because it makes booking transportation faster, easier, and more predictable.

The good news is that foreigners can use DiDi in China without a Chinese phone number or Chinese bank account. International travelers can download the DiDi app, register with an overseas phone number, use English features, and pay with supported payment methods such as international credit cards, Alipay, or WeChat Pay.

This guide explains everything you need to know about how to use DiDi in China as a foreigner, including registration, payment setup, booking rides, airport transfers, safety tips, and common problems.

## Quick Answer: Can Foreigners Use DiDi in China?

Yes. **Foreigners can use DiDi in China.**

You can:

- Download the DiDi app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store
- Register with an international phone number
- Receive an SMS verification code
- Use DiDi in major Chinese cities
- Pay with international credit cards, Alipay, or WeChat Pay
- Book rides without speaking Chinese

You do not usually need:

- A Chinese phone number
- A Chinese bank account
- A Chinese ID card

For most tourists, the easiest setup is:

**DiDi app + Alipay or WeChat Pay**

## What Is DiDi Chuxing in China?

**DiDi Chuxing** is China’s most popular ride-hailing platform, similar to Uber. It connects passengers with professional drivers, taxis, and private vehicles through a mobile app.

DiDi is widely used in major Chinese cities, including:

- Beijing
- Shanghai
- Guangzhou
- Shenzhen
- Chengdu
- Hangzhou
- Xi’an
- Chongqing

For foreign travelers, DiDi is useful because it allows you to:

- Enter destinations on a map
- See estimated prices before booking
- Avoid complicated taxi conversations
- Track your driver
- Pay digitally

Unlike traditional taxis, DiDi provides a complete booking system inside one app.

## How to Set Up DiDi as a Foreigner

Setting up DiDi before your trip will save time after arriving in China.

### 1. Download the DiDi App

You can download DiDi from:

- Apple App Store
- Google Play Store

Search for:

**DiDi**

After installing the app:

1. Open DiDi
2. Allow location permission
3. Select language options if available
4. Start account registration

A stable internet connection is recommended because DiDi requires maps and location services.

### 2. Register with an International Phone Number

Foreign visitors can usually register with an international phone number.

The setup process:

1. Select your country code
2. Enter your phone number
3. Receive the SMS verification code
4. Complete account setup

A Chinese phone number is not required for many foreign users.

If you cannot receive the SMS verification code, check:

- International roaming settings
- Phone signal
- Country code format
- Carrier restrictions

### 3. Add a Payment Method

Before booking your first ride, add a payment method.

Common options include:

- International credit cards
- Alipay
- WeChat Pay

Setting this up before arrival avoids payment problems when you need transportation.

## How to Book a DiDi Ride Step by Step in China

Once your DiDi account and payment method are ready, booking a ride is similar to using Uber or other ride-hailing apps.

The basic process is:

**Open DiDi → Confirm pickup location → Enter destination → Choose ride type → Confirm driver → Start your trip**

### Step 1: Open DiDi and Check Your Current Location

When you open DiDi, the app can automatically detect your current location through your phone’s GPS.

Make sure location permission is enabled because DiDi uses your location to:

- Find nearby drivers
- Show available vehicles
- Set pickup points
- Provide accurate navigation

This is especially important at:

- Airports
- Train stations
- Shopping malls
- Tourist attractions

### Step 2: Select Your Pickup Location

The pickup location tells the driver where to meet you.

You can use your current location or manually choose a point on the map.

For better results:

- Stand near the correct entrance
- Select the exact pickup area
- Avoid crowded areas when possible

Large locations in China often have multiple entrances. For example, an airport may have separate pickup areas for different terminals.

Always check the pickup point before confirming your ride.

### Step 3: Enter Your Destination

Enter your drop-off location in the destination field.

DiDi works with map-based searches, and many popular places can be found in English. However, smaller locations may be easier to find using Chinese names.

Before your trip, save important addresses in your notes app:

- Hotel name and address
- Train station
- Airport terminal
- Tourist attractions
- Restaurants

Having Chinese addresses available can help avoid language problems.

### Step 4: Choose Your Ride Type

DiDi offers different ride options depending on the city.

For most foreign travelers:

| Ride Type | Best For |
|---|---|
| DiDi Express | Everyday transportation and sightseeing |
| DiDi Premier | More comfortable rides and business trips |
| DiDi Luxe | High-end vehicles and special occasions |
| Taxi | Traditional taxi service through the app |

##### DiDi Express

This is the most common choice for tourists.

It is usually:

- Affordable
- Easy to find
- Available in major Chinese cities

For most travelers, Express is enough for daily transportation.

##### DiDi Premier and DiDi Luxe

These options provide a more premium experience.

They may offer:

- Higher-end cars
- More comfortable vehicles
- Professional service

They are suitable for:

- Business travelers
- Airport transfers
- Special occasions

### Step 5: Confirm the Ride

Before confirming, check:

- Pickup location
- Destination
- Estimated price
- Ride type
- Payment method

After booking, DiDi will show:

- Driver information
- Vehicle details
- License plate number
- Estimated arrival time

### Step 6: Verify the Vehicle Before Entering

Always confirm the vehicle information before getting inside.

Check:

- License plate number
- Vehicle model
- Driver information

Do not enter a car only because someone says they are your driver.

The vehicle shown in the DiDi app should match the arriving car.

## DiDi vs Traditional Taxis in China: Which Is Better for Foreign Travelers?

| Feature | DiDi | Traditional Taxi |

## Common Problems When Using DiDi in China (and Solutions)

Even though DiDi is convenient, foreign travelers may sometimes encounter problems.

### Problem 1: Cannot Receive the SMS Verification Code

If you cannot receive the DiDi verification code:

Try:

- Checking your international roaming
- Confirming your country code
- Restarting your phone connection
- Trying again later

Make sure your phone number can receive international SMS messages.

### Problem 2: DiDi Cannot Find Your Destination

Some smaller locations may not appear clearly in English.

Solutions:

- Search using Chinese names
- Copy the Chinese address
- Select the location directly from the map
- Ask your hotel for the Chinese address

Popular tourist locations are usually easier to find.

### Problem 3: Driver Cannot Find You

This commonly happens at:

- Airports
- Large shopping malls
- Train stations

Solutions:

- Check your pickup point
- Move closer to the designated pickup area
- Send a message through the app
- Share nearby landmarks

### Problem 4: Payment Does Not Work

If your payment fails:

Check:

- Linked card information
- Alipay connection
- WeChat Pay settings
- Internet connection

Having more than one payment method is recommended when traveling in China.
