## Why Digital Payments Beat Cash and Cards in China

Before diving into the AliPay vs WeChat comparison, it's important to understand why mobile payments are essential in China, and why traditional payment methods fall short.

China was one of the earliest adopters of digital payments, and has never looked back. Between AliPay and WeChat over 90% of transactions in China are conducted through digital payments. This is not just for purchases at stores, but also for paying bills, splitting restaurant tabs, and even sending money to friends. Everybody from international chain brands through to small local vendors accept mobile payments. It is much more likely that you will have a vendor not accept cash, than a vendor who won't accept mobile payments, making it the most convenient option for consumers.

### Why International Cards Fail

- • Most merchants don't accept foreign cards
- • Even when accepted, processing is slow
- • High foreign transaction fees
- • Many card readers are domestic-only

### Why Cash is Problematic

- • Many vendors refuse cash payments
- • No change available at small vendors
- • Risk of counterfeit bills
- • Inconvenient for small purchases

### Why Mobile Payments Dominate

- **Lightning Fast:** Payments complete in 2-3 seconds
- **Ultra Secure:** Biometric authentication and encryption
- **Universal:** Accepted everywhere from street food to luxury stores

- **Simple Process:** Just scan a QR code
- **Perfect Records:** Automatic receipts and transaction history
- **Any Amount:** From ¥1 street snacks to hotel bills

## How QR Code Payments Work: A Beginner's Guide

### Never Used QR Codes Before? Don't Worry!

If you've never used QR codes for payments (those square, maze-like patterns), think of them as modern barcodes that your phone camera can read instantly. The process is surprisingly simple and much faster than traditional card payments.
