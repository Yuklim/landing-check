## **What is DiDi App in China for Foreigners?**

![What is DiDi App in China for Foreigners?](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M77012000j5u4nd1987A.png)

DiDi is China's top ride-hailing app (like Uber) that foreigners can use with international phone numbers and credit cards.​​ The English version lets you book cars, bikes, and even buses across most Chinese cities, with cashless payments via Visa/Mastercard or Alipay. It's good for stress-free travel, with real-time translations and upfront pricing to avoid language barriers.

## **How to Use DiDi App - Download & Setup DiDi**

​

![How to Use DiDi App - Download & Setup DiDi](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M76212000ki59a30958C.png)

- ​**Search for “DiDi: Ride Hailing in China”​**​ – Choose the legit one. Skip random clones.
- ​**Foreign Numbers Work!​**​ No need for a Chinese SIM. Just sign up with your usual number.
- ​**Switch to English ASAP**: Go to **Settings** → **Language** → **English**.

## **How to Use DiDi App - Payment Settings**

​

![How to Use DiDi App - Payment Settings](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M72012000kgieaag2EE8.png)

### **Set Up Auto-Pay (Seriously, Do This)​**

​
Head to the **“Me”** tab → **Wallet** → **Payment Methods** → toggle on ​**auto-deduct**. No more awkwardly scrambling to pay after your ride. DiDi just quietly takes your money, like a ninja.

### **Cards That Actually Work**

​
Foreign cards like Visa, Mastercard, and even Diners Club are accepted. Prefer mobile wallets? Link ​**Alipay**​ or ​**Apple Pay**. Pro tip: If your card gets declined (thanks, bank fraud alerts), use Alipay’s **Tour Pass**—it’s a prepaid balance for tourists.

### **Cash? Only in a Pinch**

​
Some drivers accept cash, but it’s rare. Always ask **“Kěyǐ yòng xiànjīn ma?”** (Can I use cash?) before hopping in. Better yet—stick to auto-pay.

## **How to Use DiDi App - Booking Process**

​

![How to Use DiDi App - Booking Process](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M75412000kginhymAF57.png)

### **Finding Your Pickup Spot**​

- Enable GPS, but ​**double-check the pickup pin**. Streets in China can be wild (e.g., three “Starbucks” on one block).
- Type destinations in ​**English or Pinyin**​ (e.g., “Beijing Railway Station” or “Beijing Huochezhan”).

### **Picking the Right Car**​

- ​**DiDi Express**: Cheap, no-frills rides. Perfect for 90% of trips.
- ​**Premier**: Fancy cars with bottled water. Worth it for airports or post-12-hour-flight exhaustion.

### **The “Confirm Your Life Choices” Step**​

1. Tap **Confirm Request**.
2. ​**Screenshot the driver’s license plate**​ (e.g., 沪A·12345).
3. When the driver arrives, show them the ​**last 4 digits of your phone number**​ (type this in your notes app beforehand, cuz some driver don't understand English numbers).

## **How to Use DiDi App - Cancellation Rules**​

### **How to Avoid Cancellation Fees**

​
Cancel within ​**2 minutes**​ of booking—no charge. After that, fees start at ¥3-5. If the driver’s late or takes a weird route, dispute the fee in the app.

### **The “Driver Cancelled on Me” Rage**

​
It happens. Maybe your pickup spot is a maze, or they got a better fare. Don’t take it personally—just rebook and wave extra enthusiastically next time.

## **How to Use DiDi App - Good to Know Tips**

​

![How to Use DiDi App - Good to Know Tips](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M70l12000jgdl5kzA4DE.png)

Credit: Unsplash

​**SOS Button & Safety Checks**​

- Always match the car’s license plate to the app. No match? Cancel and report.
- Tap **Share Trip** to send your ride details to a friend. If things feel sketchy, hit the ​**SOS button**—it alerts local authorities.

**“Where Are You?!” (Handling Pickup Issues)​**​
Use the in-app chat’s ​**auto-translate**​ to message drivers. Example: **“I’m wearing a blue backpack near the subway exit!”** Send a voice note if typing feels slow.

**Lost & Found 101**​
Left your AirPods in the car? Use the **Lost Item** feature in the app. Offer a ¥20-50 reward via chat—it speeds things up. No response? Call DiDi support at **400-000-0999.**

**Off-Peak Hours:**Avoid 7–9 AM and 5–7 PM. Surge pricing hits hard during rush hour.

​**DiDi Express Pool**: Shared rides cost 30-50% less. You might make a local friend (or just awkwardly avoid eye contact).

## **How to Use DiDi on Alipay & WeChat**

Don’t wanna download another app? No problem. DiDi’s mini-programs in ​Alipay​ and ​WeChat​ let you book rides without cluttering your phone.

![How to Use DiDi on Alipay & WeChat](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M72k12000kgi85j45629.png)

### **How to Book DiDi Rides in Alipay**​

​**Step 1: Find the DiDi Mini-Program**​

- Open Alipay → Tap ​**​“Transport”​**​ (look for the car icon).
- Choose for ​**​“Taxi”​**​ → DiDi’ll pop up as a service.

**Step 2: Book Your Ride**​

- Same as the app: Enter pickup/destination, choose car type (Express/Premier), confirm.
- ​**Bonus**: Payment is **automatic** through your Alipay balance or linked card. No setup needed!

![How to Use DiDi on Alipay & WeChat](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M76q12000kgi7rfqF4A0.png)

### **How to Use DiDi in WeChat**​​

- Go to WeChat → ​**Discover**​ → ​**Mini Programs**​ → Search ​**​“滴滴出行”​**​ (DiDi Chuxing), paste **滴滴** in Chinese! You can't search with didi. Pay with ​**WeChat Pay**​ seamlessly.
- Use WeChat’s ​**​“Translate”​**​ tool (screenshot the page → tap “Translate”).
- ​**Pro tip**: Save these characters for car types: 快车 (Express), 专车 (Premier).

## ​**How to Use DiDi - Choose Car Type**

![​How to Use DiDi - Choose Car Type](https://dimg04.tripcdn.com/images/0M73l12000kglqu04294A.png)

Picking the right ride on DiDi is like choosing between instant noodles (cheap and reliable) and a fancy hotpot (splurge-worthy). Here’s the lowdown:

### ​**Express (快车)​**​

- ​**What you get**: Basic cars (think Toyota/Hyundai), no frills.
- ​**Price**: Cheapest. A 30-minute ride in Beijing ≈ ¥35-50.
- ​**Best for**: Solo travelers, short trips, or when you’re pretending to be on a budget.

### ​**Premier (专车)​**​

- ​**What you get**: Cleaner cars (often Buick or VW), free water, quieter drivers.
- ​**Price**: 20-30% pricier than Express. Same ride ≈ ¥60-80.
- ​**Best for**: Airport runs, business trips, or when you **need** AC to survive summer.

### ​**Luxe (豪华车)​**​

- ​**What you get**: Mercedes-Benz, umbrellas, phone chargers, drivers in suits.
- ​**Price**: 2-3x Express. Flex for special occasions.
- ​**Best for**: Impressing your date or drowning in luxury after a brutal flight delay.

### ​**Express Pool (拼车)​**​

- ​**What you get**: Shared rides with strangers (awkward silence included).
- ​**Price**: 30-50% off Express.
- ​**Best for**: Saving cash when you’re **not** in a hurry.

​**Pro Tip**: After selecting a car type, ​**swipe left**​ to see all options. Some cities even offer vans or EVs!

## ​**How Much Does DiDi Cost**

​
DiDi’s pricing is straightforward, but a few quirks can catch travelers off guard. Here’s what you need to know:

- ​**Base Fare**: Starts at ​**​¥10-15**​ (varies by city).
- ​**Per Kilometer**: ​**​¥2-4**​ (cheaper than traditional taxis).
- ​**Per Minute**: ​**​¥0.5-1**​ during traffic jams.
- ​**Surge Pricing**: Avoid ​**7-9 AM**​ and ​**5-7 PM**​ (weekdays) when fares spike up to 2x.

​**Real-World Example**:
A 20-minute, 8km ride in Beijing:

- ​**Express**: ¥30-40
- ​**Premier**: ¥50-70
- ​**Luxe**: ¥100+

## FAQs about DiDi App

- Can a foreigner use DiDi in China?

 Yes! Foreigners can use DiDi with their passport and international phone number. The app has English language support.
- Do I need a China number to use DiDi?​

 No. You can register with any international number, but you'll need to enable roaming to receive the SMS verification code when signing up.
- ​How do I pay with DiDi app in China?​

 You can pay by:
International credit cards (Visa/Mastercard)
Alipay Tour Pass
WeChat Pay (if connected to foreign card)
Cash (though most drivers prefer cashless)
- Does DiDi work everywhere in China?​

 DiDi works in most cities (including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Chengdu), but availability decreases in remote rural areas. Major tourist destinations are all covered.
- Is DiDi cheaper than taxis in China?

 Generally, DiDi is cheaper than regular taxis in China. But it really depends on which service you pick. For daily rides, Express is your best budget option. Premier costs more and is more of a premium choice.
- Is DiDi safe for foreigners?

 If you don’t speak Chinese, DiDi (China’s version of Uber) has you covered. In-app translation, safety tools, and saved addresses make it super easy to get around cities quickly and safely. Want to know more? Check out our guide on using DiDi as a foreigner.
