## **What is DiDi?**

![DiDi 'Ride in China' graphic with the DiDi logo, YouTrip panda mascots and a cartoon car](https://www.you.co/sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/06/blog-vertical-1-2.png)

![DiDi app screen showing auto-translated instant messaging with a driver who is on the way](https://www.you.co/sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/06/blog-vertical-2-1.png)

*Image Credits: Google Play Store*

DiDi Chuxing (滴滴出行) is China’s largest ride-hailing platform — think of it as China’s version of Grab or Gojek. Founded in 2012, DiDi now offers a wide range of transportation services, including:

- Private cars and taxis
- Hitch (carpooling)
- Chauffeur services
- Bike and e-scooter rentals
- Intercity rides
- Delivery and logistics

It’s used by hundreds of millions of people across China and has even expanded globally to Latin America, Japan, and Australia.

Alternatively, use the search bar on the homepage to search for DD or DiDi Chuxing.
3. **Grant the mini-program permission to access your location**

## **Step-by-Step Guide: Booking a Ride**

![How to book a DiDi ride in China — step-by-step infographic](https://www.you.co/sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/06/1080-x-1440-latest-new-IG-size-1-1-768x1024.png)

1. **Open the DiDi mini program in WeChat**
2. **Enter your pickup and drop-off locations**
The interface is in English, but you may have to type your address in Chinese for more accurate results — or just drop a pin if the location name doesn’t appear.
3. **Step 3: Pick a ride type**
Budget is Discount Express; Standard covers Express, DiDi Flash and Taxi; Premium is Select and Premier; and there are 6-Seater and Luxe 6-Seater options too. Prices are shown upfront before you book.
4. **Step 4: Book and track your ride**
The app shows your driver’s name, plate number, and estimated arrival time, with in-app translation tools to help you communicate with your driver.

## **Is DiDi Cheaper Than a Taxi?**

Yes, in most cases, DiDi is more affordable than flagging down a local taxi in China.

DiDi tends to work out cheaper than metered taxis, especially in urban areas. Plus, with upfront pricing, there’s no risk of meter scams or route manipulation 👀

## **Key Features of the DiDi App**

- **International usage:** supports international phone numbers and cards; the app runs in English with in-app Chinese-English message translation; the separate DiDi Global app works in select overseas markets (e.g. Mexico, Brazil, Japan).

- **WeChat integration:** seamless bookings within WeChat Mini Programs; easy payments using WeChat Pay; trip updates via WeChat messages.

- **Tech & safety:** SOS emergency button; real-time GPS tracking; facial recognition to verify drivers; driver background checks and in-app ratings.

- **Other services:** DiDi Bike (rent e-bikes and scooters in major cities); DiDi Luxe (premium cars with trained chauffeurs); DiDi Bus (ride-sharing on fixed shuttle routes); DiDi Food (meal delivery, China-only).

## **Tips for First-Time Riders**

- Use the hotel WiFi if you don’t have mobile data.
- In rural areas, ride availability may be limited — book ahead or expect longer waiting times.
- Screenshot your ride details before you board.
