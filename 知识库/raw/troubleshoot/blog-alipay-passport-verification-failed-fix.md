## Why Alipay Passport Verification Fails

Alipay's identity verification uses AI-powered OCR to read your passport. When it fails, it is almost always a scanning environment problem — not a problem with your passport itself. Here are the main causes:

Holographic glare on passport data page (most common)

Every modern passport has a holographic security overlay. Under normal lighting, this creates reflections that block the OCR from reading the MRZ (Machine Readable Zone) code at the bottom of your data page.

MRZ code not readable due to reflection or shadow

Even without glare, shadows from your hand or phone can obscure the two-line MRZ code that Alipay needs to read.

NFC chip issues

Older passports may not have an NFC chip. Phone cases — especially magnetic or wallet cases — can block the NFC signal entirely.

Name mismatch between app entry and passport

If you typed your name differently than it appears on the MRZ line (e.g., middle name included vs. excluded), the verification will reject it.

VPN running during verification

Alipay flags VPN connections as suspicious. This can cause verification to silently fail or trigger a security lockout.

Too many failed attempts — temporary lockout

After repeated failures, Alipay temporarily locks you out for approximately 3 hours. This auto-releases — do not reinstall the app during this time.

Expired passport

Alipay checks your passport expiry date. An expired passport will always fail verification.

## Common Error Messages

Here are the exact error messages users encounter during Alipay passport verification, and what each one means:

Generic failure — usually means the passport scan could not read your MRZ code. Try the dark background fix below.

Temporary lockout. Wait approximately 3 hours before trying again. Do not reinstall the app.

Facial recognition could not match your live face to your passport photo. See Fix #4 below for tips.

## The "Dark Background" Fix (Most Effective)

This single technique solves the problem for most users.

The holographic security overlay on your passport data page reflects light in a way that confuses Alipay's OCR scanner. A dark, matte background absorbs that reflected light instead of bouncing it back into the camera.

Find a dark, matte surface — a black t-shirt, dark folder, dark bedsheet, or black notebook cover. Avoid glossy or reflective dark surfaces.

Place your passport open on the data page, flat on that surface. Press gently to keep it flat.

Move to a dimly lit room with indirect natural light. Near a window (but not in direct sunlight) works best. Turn off overhead fluorescent lights.

Hold your phone camera parallel to the passport — not at an angle. Keep about 15-20 cm distance.

Make sure all 4 corners of the passport data page are visible with a small margin around each edge.

Check that you can read every character in both MRZ lines (the two lines of letters, numbers, and chevrons <<< at the bottom of the page).

Keep your fingers out of the shot completely — no thumbs holding the passport open.

Why this works

The holographic security overlay on modern passports reflects ambient light at multiple angles. On a white or light surface, that reflected light bounces back into the camera, creating bright spots that obscure the printed text and MRZ code. A dark, matte surface absorbs this light instead. Dim, indirect lighting further reduces the holographic effect. Together, they give Alipay's OCR a clean, high-contrast image to read.

## Fix #2: NFC Scanning Technique

If Alipay asks you to scan your passport via NFC, here is how to get it to work reliably on the first try.

Check if your passport has NFC: look for the e-passport symbol (a small chip icon) on the front cover. Most passports issued after 2006 have it.

Find your phone's NFC antenna position. It varies by model: iPhones have it at the top-back; Samsung phones near the center-back; Google Pixel phones near the top-back near the camera.

Remove your phone case — especially magnetic cases, wallet cases, or cases with metal plates. These block NFC signals.

Toggle NFC off and back on in your phone settings before attempting the scan.

Close all other NFC-using apps (Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, transit card apps).

Open your passport to the data page. Place your phone flat against it, with the NFC antenna area directly over the passport chip. Move slowly in small circles until the app responds.

NFC not working at all?

If your passport does not have an NFC chip (pre-2006 passports from many countries), you can skip this step entirely. Alipay offers manual entry and photo upload as alternatives.

## Fix #3: Manual Entry Alternative

If both the camera scan and NFC fail, Alipay allows you to enter your passport details manually. This bypasses all scanning issues entirely.

Manual entry fields:

- • **Full name** — exactly as it appears on your passport MRZ line (surname first)
- • **Passport number**
- • **Date of birth**
- • **Expiry date**
- • **Number of renewals** — if your passport has been renewed, enter the count (usually 0 or 1)

You can also **upload a pre-taken photo** from your gallery instead of using the live camera. Take the photo separately using your phone's default camera app (with the dark background technique above), then select it from your gallery when Alipay prompts for the scan.

Common mistake: name format

Enter your name exactly as it appears on the MRZ line of your passport — not as it appears on the printed text above. The MRZ line uses a specific format: SURNAME<<FIRSTNAME<MIDDLENAME. Mismatches between your typed name and MRZ data are a top cause of manual entry failures.

## Fix #4: Face Recognition Tips

After scanning your passport, Alipay requires a live facial recognition check. This step compares your live face to the photo stored in your passport. Here is how to pass it on the first attempt:

Remove glasses, hats, masks, and sunglasses

Even clear prescription glasses can cause reflections that confuse the recognition algorithm.

Face a window with natural daylight

Front-facing natural light creates the even illumination the algorithm needs. Avoid overhead lights that cast shadows under your eyes and nose.

Clean your phone's front camera lens

Smudges reduce image clarity. Wipe with a soft cloth before starting.

Hold steady until the process completes

Do not move your head or blink excessively. Keep a neutral expression and look directly at the camera.

Appearance changed significantly?

If you look very different from your passport photo (significant weight change, different hairstyle, aged considerably), the facial recognition may struggle. This is a known difficulty. Try manual entry as an alternative, or contact Alipay support for assistance.

## Fix #5: VPN and Network Issues

Running a VPN during verification is one of the most common hidden causes of failure. Alipay's security system flags VPN IP addresses as potentially suspicious, which can silently block verification or trigger the "malicious activity" error.

Disable your VPN completely before starting verification

This includes system-wide VPNs, VPN apps running in the background, and DNS-based VPNs (like 1.1.1.1 WARP).

Use mobile data instead of hotel/public Wi-Fi

Some hotel Wi-Fi networks route through VPN-like proxies. Use your roaming mobile data or a local Chinese SIM for the most direct connection.

Turn your VPN back on only after verification completes

Once your identity is verified, using a VPN for day-to-day Alipay payments does not cause issues.

## Fix #6: App Reset and Updates

If the above fixes do not work, a stale app cache or outdated version may be the problem.

Steps:

- • **Update Alipay** to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play. Older versions may have known verification bugs.
- • **Clear Alipay app cache** (Android: Settings > Apps > Alipay > Storage > Clear Cache).
- • **On iOS**: delete the Alipay app and reinstall it. iOS does not allow clearing individual app caches.
- • **Restart your phone** after reinstalling — this resets NFC and camera permissions.

Do not reinstall during a lockout

If you see "too many attempts, please try again later," do not delete and reinstall the app. The lockout is server-side (tied to your account, not your device). Reinstalling will not help and may add further complications. Wait the full 3 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my Alipay passport scan keep failing?

The most common cause is holographic glare on your passport data page. The security overlay reflects light and confuses Alipay's OCR scanner. Place your passport on a dark, matte surface (like a black t-shirt) in a dimly lit room with indirect natural light. This eliminates the glare and lets the AI read your MRZ code.

Can I verify my Alipay before arriving in China?

Yes, it is recommended to verify before you travel. Download Alipay, complete identity verification with your passport, and [link your card](/guides/using-alipay) before departure. However, some users report that verification only completes successfully once they are physically in China. If it fails abroad, try again after landing.

What if my passport doesn't have an NFC chip?

You can skip NFC scanning entirely. Alipay offers manual entry (type your passport details) and photo upload (take or upload a photo of your passport data page) as alternative verification methods. Most passports issued before 2006 do not have NFC chips.

How long does Alipay verification take?

Most verifications complete within minutes to 1 hour. Officially, Alipay states it can take up to 24 hours. If it has been longer than 24 hours, contact Alipay support at 95188 (within China) or +86 571 26886000 (international).

I got locked out after too many attempts. How long until I can try again?

The lockout auto-releases after approximately 3 hours. Do not uninstall or reinstall the app during this period — it will not speed up the process. The lockout is server-side. Wait the full 3 hours, then try again with the dark background technique.

Does Alipay accept any ID other than passport?

For foreign tourists, only passports are accepted for identity verification on Alipay. Chinese national ID cards work for Chinese citizens, but foreign driving licenses, national ID cards, or other documents are not supported.

Need help with the next step?

Once your passport verification is complete, set up your payment methods and get ready to pay everywhere in China.
